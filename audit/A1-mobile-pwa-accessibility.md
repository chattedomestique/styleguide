# A1: Mobile PWA, accessibility, interaction

## 1. Rules to adopt into the guidelines

### §2 Foundations › Hit targets and focus
- Every control a finger can reach is `--hit` (44px) in both axes, foot links included. A smaller mark sits inside an invisible 44px box. Links inside a sentence are the only exception. [interaction-design] [ui-ux-pro-max]
- A ring takes the ink of the surface it lands on: inset rings use that surface's `*-ink` role (`--card-bar-ink` in a bar), outside rings use `--focus`. Test every tone, `ink` included. [web-design-guidelines]
- A sticky or fixed bar sets `scroll-padding-block-start` (or `-end`) to its block size plus its safe-area inset, so a focused element never ends up under the bar (WCAG 2.4.11). [web-design-guidelines]

### §3 Card › States
- `:hover` only inside `@media (hover: hover)` (`.is-hover` outside). Every control has an `:active` look (`--fill: 1` or ink↔paper); then `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent`. [web-design-guidelines] [ui-ux-pro-max]

### §3 Card › Responsive behaviour
- Card rows wrap (`.card__bar, .card__row, .card__foot, .card--ghost { flex-wrap: wrap }`); `.t-display` is capped at `min(var(--text-4xl), 19vw)`. Gate: 320px at 200% text, no clipping. [web-design-guidelines] [interaction-design]

### §3 Card › Accessibility
- A collection of cards is `<ul class="card-grid" role="list">` with one `<li>` per card. Grid selectors target `> li` or `> *`, never only `> .card`. [web-design-guidelines]
- A card title sits one level below its section heading (an h2 section takes h3 titles). Each card has one heading. A bar label is the heading only when the card has no `.card__title`. [web-design-guidelines]
- A disabled card states the reason in visible text (`.card__meta`: "Unlocks after Colours"). In forced colours it is drawn in `GrayText`. [interaction-design]
- Meters carry `aria-valuetext` in the card's own words ("5 of 12 learned"). Hints work for every input: "Turn over", not "Tap to flip". Every gesture (swipe, long-press) also has a visible button. [interaction-design]
- `@media (prefers-contrast: more)`: `--ink-soft` and `--line-soft` become `var(--ink)`; `.card { --card-ink-soft: var(--card-ink) }`. Reduced transparency needs no rule while rule 2 holds. [interaction-design]

### Platforms: PWA
- Viewport meta adds `viewport-fit=cover`; tokens `--safe-top|right|bottom|left: env(safe-area-inset-*, 0px)` are read by bars and the app frame only (`max(var(--gutter), var(--safe-left))`), never cards. [ui-ux-pro-max]
- `theme-color` and manifest `background_color` = `--canvas` (`check.mjs` compares). `dvh` for the app frame only; size-once uses `svh`: `.card--study { max-inline-size: min(22rem, (100svh - var(--chrome, 0px)) * 5 / 7) }`. [ui-ux-pro-max]
- base.css: `:is(input, select, textarea) { font-size: max(1rem, 1em) }` (under 16px, iOS zooms on focus). Inner scrollers get `overscroll-behavior: contain`; refresh is a button, not pull-to-refresh. [web-design-guidelines] [ui-ux-pro-max]
- Standalone has no browser back or refresh: every screen past the root shows a back control. Empty = ghost card; offline/error = plain card with "Try again" in its foot; never blank. [ui-ux-pro-max] [interaction-design]
- iOS larger text needs an opt-in: `@supports (font: -apple-system-body) { html { font: -apple-system-body } }` (root becomes 17px). Owner's call; test at the largest size. [interaction-design]

## 2. Conflicts

1. **Reduced motion.** web-design-guidelines (6.1) and interaction-design (accessibility-patterns) say that under `prefers-reduced-motion`, every animation and transition becomes `0.01ms !important`. We say (STYLE.md §2 Motion) "reduced motion means gentler, not instant". Recommendation: **keep ours.** The same skill's animation-timing reference keeps fast opacity changes (under 150ms), and a global kill switch would also remove the crossfade that replaces the flip. Gate 4 should make it concrete: the flip becomes an opacity crossfade of 150ms or less, the lift keeps its shadow but drops the translate, and focus rings are always instant.
2. **44px, and the WCAG number cited for it.** web-design-guidelines (2.5) calls 44×44 "WCAG 2.5.8" and puts `min-height: 44px` on every `a, button, input`. We use 44px on controls but 24px on foot links (STYLE.md:340-341). Recommendation: **adopt 44px for every touch control** (A1-04), because platform conventions (Apple's 44pt, Material's 48dp) outrank the legal minimum. But cite the platforms or 2.5.5 (AAA): 2.5.8 sets 24px. Leave links inside sentences alone, since WCAG exempts inline links.
3. **What disabled looks like.** interaction-design (component-patterns) says disabled means reduced opacity. We use a dashed frame, `--ink-faint` and a hatch, and STYLE.md says "Don't fake disabled with opacity". Recommendation: **keep ours.** Opacity also fades the frame and lets the surface show through, which is the translucency rule 2 bans, and it disappears in forced colours. Take that pattern's other half instead: explain why (A1-10).
4. **Floating navbar.** ui-ux-pro-max (Common Rules) says to float the navbar (`top-4 left-4 right-4`) rather than stick it to `top-0`. Our bars run edge to edge ("the frame made thicker"; `.sg-top`). Recommendation: **keep ours** for the coming Nav bar. In an installed PWA the bar has to own the safe-area inset. A floating bar shows content scrolling behind it, which needs a blur or shadow to separate it (both banned), and it costs about 32px on a 667px phone.
5. **Instant state changes.** ui-ux-pro-max (Pre-Delivery Checklist, Common Rules) wants 150–300ms transitions and lists "instant state changes" as a don't. We change states instantly until gate 4. Recommendation: **keep ours for now.** Motion is gated, and instant is the correct reduced-motion fallback anyway. At gate 4, use `--dur-press` (130ms) and `--dur-hover` (200ms); both already fall within the skill's range, so no token changes.

## 4. Skill entries for "Skills we build with"

**web-design-guidelines**
- Load: when writing or reviewing an element's HTML/CSS, and for each gate's keyboard and touch pass.
- Take: §1 (focus, headings, skip link, semantics), §2.5–2.7 (targets, viewport, reflow), §9 (hover parity, tap highlight, touch-action), §4.6 (heading order).
- Ignore: its sample colours and soft-shadow card hover; the global reduced-motion kill switch; "44px = WCAG 2.5.8" (2.5.8 is 24px); the React and performance sections.

**interaction-design** (SKILL.md plus references/accessibility-patterns, component-patterns, input-and-physicality, animation-timing)
- Load: when specifying an element's states, gestures or empty/error states, and at gate 4 (motion).
- Take: a state table per element; a visible alternative for every gesture; "explain why" for disabled; empty and error states; reduced motion as a fade under 150ms. Its duration bands match our `--dur-*` tokens.
- Ignore: haptics, VR, eye tracking, marking menus, springs and overshoot, "Disabled: reduced opacity", and the essays on dramatic arc.

**ui-ux-pro-max**
- Load: only as a final checklist pass on a finished screen. Never run its generate-a-design-system workflow or its scripts.
- Take: Quick Reference 1–4 (touch-target-size, hover-vs-tap, viewport-meta, readable-font-size, horizontal-scroll), and `data/ux-guidelines.csv` rows 17 (fixed positioning and safe areas), 20 (dvh), 23 (8px spacing between targets) and 26 (overscroll).
- Ignore: its styles, palettes and font pairings; the floating navbar and glass advice; Tailwind-only rules ("bg-primary, not var()"); "instant state changes" counted as a defect before gate 4; its icon-set and emoji rules.
