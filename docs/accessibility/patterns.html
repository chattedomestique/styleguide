Accessibility

Keyboard patterns

Every element, the pattern it follows and the keys it answers to. The keys come from each element's own accessibility table and from its script; the spec in the last line of each pattern cell is the file that presses them in a real browser.

The rules every element follows

  • Native first. A <button>, <a>, <input>, <select>, <details>, <dialog>, popover attribute, <progress> and <input type="range"> where one exists. A custom role only where there is no native element, following the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices (APG) pattern exactly.
  • One tab stop per group. Tabs, radio groups, toolbars, menus and grids use a roving tabindex: Tab enters on the current item and leaves on the next press; arrow keys move inside.
  • Arrow keys and selection. In a radio group, tabs (automatic) and the segmented control, the arrow moves focus and selects. In a toolbar, a timeline track and a menu, it moves focus only; Enter or Space acts.
  • Esc closes, and focus goes back. Every overlay closes on Esc and returns focus to what opened it. There is always a visible button as well, because a touch screen has no Esc. No keyboard traps.
  • Mirrored in right-to-left. Left and Right swap wherever they mean "previous" and "next".
  • Unavailable is not gone. aria-disabled="true" keeps a control focusable so a person can find out why, and SG.guard makes it inert for clicks and keys. A native disabled control is skipped.
  • Every gesture has a tap equivalent. Sliders, carousels, sheets, timelines and toolbars have buttons or click-to-jump, and hold-to-repeat is an enhancement over a tap.
The keys used across the guide and what each is for
KeyUsed for
Tab, Shift+TabMove between tab stops; leave a group.
EnterFollow a link, press a button, submit a form, confirm; choose a menu row or a day.
SpacePress a button; toggle a checkbox or switch; check a radio; choose a day or a clip.
EscClose an overlay, dismiss a tooltip or toast, clear a search field; focus returns.
Left Right Up DownMove inside a group: tabs, radios, toolbar, menu, calendar, carousel, timeline; step a slider or spinbutton.
Home, EndFirst and last item, or minimum and maximum.
PageUp, PageDownA larger step: a tenth of a slider, ten timeline steps, a month in a calendar.
Delete, BackspaceRemove a chip; delete a keypad digit.
F8Jump to the newest toast (the only global key).
LettersType-ahead inside an open menu or a native select; digits inside the keypad. Never global.

Groups with arrow keys

One tab stop for the group; arrow keys move inside it; Tab leaves.

Overlays and messages

Native <dialog> and popover bring focus return and Esc with them. A modal never traps without a way out.

Overlays and messages: pattern, keys and focus per element
ElementPattern and specKeysTab stops and focus
DialogAPG dialog (modal) on a native <dialog>; alertdialog for a destructive confirmation. dialog.mjsTab Shift+Tab cycle inside; Esc closes and returns focus to the opener (not in a required dialog, which has a button for every way out).Initial focus: Cancel for a destructive dialog, the primary button for a confirmation, the first field for a form. The page behind is inert.
SheetA modal or non-modal panel; the handle is a button, not a gesture. sheet.mjsEsc closes and returns focus; on the handle Enter or Space toggles full height, Up full, Down half.Modal: focus starts on Close and cycles inside. Non-modal: the page stays live and the panel follows its button in the DOM.
PopoverNative popover (auto), non-modal. popover.mjsEnter or Space on the button opens it and moves focus in; Esc closes and returns focus; a click outside closes it.Tab through its controls, then past the last one, focus moves on and it closes. Nothing behind is inert.
TooltipAPG tooltip (WCAG 1.4.13: hoverable, dismissible, persistent). tooltip.mjsFocus shows it at once; blur hides it; Esc hides it without moving focus, and the next Esc does what it did before.Never focusable, never a control's only name, never shown on touch.
ToastA persistent live region (polite; assertive for errors). toast.mjsF8 jumps to the newest toast from anywhere; Esc on a focused toast dismisses it and returns focus; Enter or Space on its buttons.Not focusable itself; its buttons are the last tab stops on the page. The timer pauses on hover, focus and a held touch.
AlertA card with role="status" or role="alert", or none. alert.mjsEnter or Space on the dismiss button hides it and moves focus to the next focusable element, never to the page.Actions, then the dismiss button. The alert itself is not focusable.

Navigation and links

Ordinary navigation: native links and buttons in visual order, no arrow-key model, no role="menu" or tablist.

Navigation and links: pattern, keys and focus per element
ElementPattern and specKeysTab stops and focus
App barA banner landmark with the page heading; the search bar is a search landmark. appbar.mjsTab through Back, the title, then actions; Enter or Space activate; Enter in the search field submits.Visual order is DOM order. A sticky bar publishes --appbar-h so focus is never hidden behind it.
BreadcrumbAPG breadcrumb: a labelled nav around an ordered list; aria-current="page" on the last item. breadcrumb.mjsTab through the links in trail order; Enter follows one; Enter or Space on "Earlier levels" shows the hidden ones.Every link is a stop. No arrow-key model.
DockA named navigation landmark of links (or buttons that swap views), with aria-current. dock.mjsTab through the items in visual order; Enter (link) or Enter and Space (button).Every item is a stop. A dock publishes --dock-h.
PaginationA labelled nav of links; the current page has aria-current. pagination.mjsTab through Previous, the numbers and Next; Enter follows a link.Previous and Next are aria-disabled at the ends and stay focusable. In a single-page app, move focus to the results heading and announce the position.
ListRows of links or buttons; role="list" keeps the list in Safari. list.mjsEnter follows a link; Enter or Space activates a button row.One tab stop per pressable row; the ring is drawn inside the row. Static rows are skipped.
TileA link or a button; a toggle uses aria-pressed. tile.mjsEnter follows; Enter or Space activates.One tab stop per tile, in DOM order.
AvatarA static image with a text name, or a link or button. avatar.mjsEnter or Space on a pressable avatar.A static avatar is not a stop; a pressable one is. The overflow button of a stack is a stop.
AgendaDay cards labelled by date, with events as a list of links that state their own times. agenda.mjsEnter follows an event.One stop per event, in time order (DOM order is time order, whatever the lane).
GalleryA list of tiles; a linked tile is one link. gallery.mjsEnter follows a tile.One stop per linked tile, in DOM order.

Status and static elements

Nothing to operate, so nothing takes focus. What they need is a name, a value in words and an announcement when it changes.

Status and static elements: pattern, keys and focus per element
ElementPattern and specKeysTab stops and focus
TagText in a pill. tag.mjsNone.Not a stop. If it must be reachable it is a chip or a link.
DividerA native <hr>, or a labelled divider that is text. divider.mjsNone.Not focusable.
LegendA list of a chart's keys and values. legend.mjsNone.Not focusable; the swatches are decorative.
SparklineOne image with a sentence for a name. sparkline.mjsNone.Not focusable; no hover-only detail.
Donut chartOne image that states the total and every part; the legend repeats them as text. donut.mjsNone.Not focusable.
ProgressNative <progress> and <meter> with a name and the value as text. progress.mjsNone.Not focusable. Milestones go through SG.announce, never every tick.
Spinnerrole="status" with a text name; an overlay makes the content beneath inert. spinner.mjsNone.Not focusable; an overlay never covers a Cancel control.
Skeletonaria-hidden shapes inside a region with aria-busy; one polite announcement when the content lands. skeleton.mjsNone.Nothing in it is focusable.
Empty stateA heading, a message and buttons, in order. empty.mjsEnter or Space on its buttons.When a state replaces focused content, move focus on purpose.
MarqueeA strip with a Pause and Play toggle (APG toggle button) before the moving text. marquee.mjsSpace or Enter on the toggle pauses and resumes; its name never changes.The toggle is reachable; the looped copy is aria-hidden and inert. Entering the strip freezes it.

Adding an element

A new element takes its pattern from the APG or from a native element, never invents one. The work is in CLAUDE.md: copy src/components/button.css, write the keyboard and screen-reader table on its page, write tests/components/<name>.mjs that presses the keys and asserts focus, ARIA state and the --lift and --fill numbers, and add a row here. The checks that prove the rest are on Testing, and the rows of the definition of done say what each has to show.

What none of this proves is what a screen reader says. Those scripts are on Manual, and nobody has run them.
