Components

Gallery

Many pictures at once, none more important than the rest by default. They sit on one grid of square cells; a few are allowed to be bigger. There is no masonry to measure and nothing to lay out in script: the browser packs it, and the DOM order is the reading order.

When to use

Do

Use it for a pile of pictures with no order: a week of photos, a portfolio, the contents of an album. Make one or two tiles bigger so the eye has somewhere to start. Mix in a card when a number or a note belongs with the pictures.

Don’t

Use it for things with a sequence (steps, a story): packing can move a small tile up beside a big one, so it will not read in order. Use a Carousel or a list. Do not make every tile big; then nothing is.

Examples

Basic

A gallery is a .card-grid with the .gallery class. Each cell is an <li>; its data-size says how many cells it takes. Every tile fills its cell, and the picture is cropped to it: set the focal point with --pos on the img when the subject is not in the middle. Tap a tile, and it goes where the app sends it (there is no lightbox here).

The columns are planned, not “as many as fit”: two on a phone, four from 32rem, one under 16rem (a phone at large text). A mosaic only looks finished when its last row is full, so the tiles are planned for two and four columns: one big (4 cells), two wide and two tall (2 each) and four singles make 16 cells, four full rows of four or eight of two. The two tall tiles stand side by side, so neither leaves a hole beside it.

Tile sizes
data-size (on the li)TakesNotes
(none)1 × 1 cellA square.
wide2 × 1A banner: a stat, a panorama, a quote. In one column it is a 2:1 banner across the whole gallery.
tall1 × 2A portrait. Put it beside another tall tile, or beside two singles. In one column it is a square.
big2 × 2The feature. In two columns it is the whole width; in one, a single square, so a span can never invent a column that does not fit.

Count the cells: big 4, wide and tall 2, a single 1. A total that is a multiple of four closes every row in two and in four columns.

Tiled

The card grid’s tiled variant drops the gap and lets neighbouring tiles share their 2px frames, so the gallery reads as one ruled sheet. Corners go square inside it, as they do for cards.

States

A tile that is still loading or has failed keeps its cell, so the gallery never reflows under you (the cell grows only when large text needs more room than it gives, because words are never cut off). In a tile under 192px the failed message drops the icon and keeps the sentence and the button; a bigger tile keeps all three, at any text size. See Media for what sets them.

Anatomy

  1. Grid ul.card-grid.gallery with role="list" and an aria-label that says what and how many. A size container, so spans respond to the gallery’s width and not the screen’s.
  2. Cell li: a square (aspect-ratio: 1) on four tracks, spanning four, two or one of them (one, two or four columns). data-size widens or lengthens it. grid-auto-flow: dense fills the holes.
  3. Tile: a filled Media figure (the ratio is ignored, the cell sets the size), or a Card that fills the cell.
  4. Caption: best as data-caption="over" in a small tile, so it does not eat the picture; bar in a tiled big tile.
  5. Gap: --space-3 (12px). Or none, with .card-grid--tiled.

Accessibility

Gallery keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
TabOne stop per linked tile, in DOM order. The focus ring goes around the whole tile, and a neighbour’s frame never covers it.
Screen reader"This week, eight photos and a count, list, 9 items." Each tile: the link’s text, and the picture’s alt. Give a picture that is the only content of a link an alt that says where it goes ("Open photo: morning bread").
Reading orderThe DOM order. dense packing can place a later small tile beside an earlier big one, so do not use a gallery for a sequence (WCAG 1.3.2).
TouchA linked tile’s hit area is the whole tile; the smallest is about 116px.
Zoom / text sizeCells are fractions of the container, captions are rem and wrap. The column breakpoints are rem, so large text gives fewer, bigger cells: at 320px wide a gallery is two columns, and one at 200% text.
Reduced motionNo lift travel; the fill still changes.
Forced coloursEvery tile keeps its real frame; a hovered or focused tile gets a 4px Highlight outline.

Tokens used

Everything in Media and Card, plus --space-3 for the gap and --bw for the tiled overlap.
