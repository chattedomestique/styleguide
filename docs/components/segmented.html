Components

Segmented control

A segmented control sets one value out of a few that are equally likely: Weekly | Monthly, Bank account | Transactions. It is a pill track with the chosen option filled in.

When to use

Do

Use two to four options with short labels, and one always chosen. It changes how the content beneath it is shown, or a setting, straight away.

Don't

Use it to navigate (links in a dock), to swap panels of different content (tabs), for five or more options, or for a choice that can be empty (chips).

Examples

Basic

Native radio inputs in a <fieldset> with a <legend>. There is no script: Tab enters the group once, on the chosen option; Left and Right move and choose; the value posts with a form. The legend can be visually hidden when the control is labelled by the text next to it.

Show

Three and four options, with icons

Options share the width equally while their labels fit, and choosing one never reflows the row (the weight does not change). When the whole labels cannot share one row (large text, a narrow column) the control does not wrap raggedly: it puts every option on a line of its own, or, with four options, two rows of two. Past four options, use a menu or chips. An icon goes before the word, never instead of it, and is left out at four options, where there is no room for both.

Sizes and full width

Small is 40px tall, medium 48, large 56. Every option keeps a 44px hit area. A control is as wide as its options need; data-block spans the container, which is what you want on a phone.

Sliding thumb

data-indicator="slide" replaces the per-option fill with one thumb that glides to the chosen option. Under reduced motion the thumb stays out of the way and each option fades its own fill in: a fade, never a jump cut. The thumb glides along one row of equal columns, so keep the labels short; when large text leaves a label no room in its column, the control stacks and each option fills itself, as under reduced motion.

Theme

Inside a tonal card

data-variant="tone" reads the tone of data-tone on the control itself, so it matches the card it sits in. This is the weekly / monthly toggle of a stat card.

States

The solid fill is kept for the chosen option. Hover draws the option's own frame (an outline, because a tint would move the text's contrast); a press tints it at once, before the radio has flipped; the chosen one is filled, with a frame of its own; keyboard focus rings the chosen option from inside (where a radio group's focus always lands), in the colour of the thumb's words, so it never runs into the track's frame; an unavailable one is dashed and skipped by the arrow keys. Each state is shown in a track of its own here, beside a plain option at rest. The is-* classes on the label only force a state for this page.

Anatomy

  1. Track (.segmented): a pill with a 2px --line frame, filled with --paper-2, 4px of padding. A <fieldset> with a <legend>, or a role="radiogroup" with a visible label.
  2. Option (.segmented__opt): a <label> holding a native radio. Equal width.
  3. Radio: invisible, covering the option and stretching to 44px tall. All keyboard and form behaviour is the browser's.
  4. Label (.segmented__label): the visible cell. Its fill follows --fill (0 empty, 0.15 pressed, 1 chosen). Optional icon, then the word, side by side.
  5. Chosen cue: a filled pill with a 2px frame of its own inside the track. The frame is the line colour, so it shows as an edge on a tone or a pale accent and merges with an ink fill. The weight does not change, so the row cannot reflow. Never colour alone.
  6. Thumb (optional, data-indicator="slide"): one pill that glides between options.

Accessibility

A native radio group (APG Radio Group), so the browser supplies the behaviour below.

Segmented control keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
Tab / Shift+TabEnters the group once, on the chosen option, and leaves on the next press.
Left / Right (and Up / Down)Move to the previous / next option and choose it, wrapping; unavailable options are skipped.
SpaceChooses the focused option (when none is chosen).
Screen reader"Show, group. Bank account, radio button, 1 of 2, selected." The legend names the group.
TouchEach option is at least 44×44px, even in the small size, and the whole cell taps, not only the word.
Zoom / text sizeThe words grow with the text, and so does an icon beside a word (1.1em); the track's padding and the side padding stop at their 100% size. The options stay in one row of equal cells while their whole labels fit (the side padding also eases down to 8px on a narrow screen). When they cannot, the control changes as a whole, measured by src/js/44-segmented.js: every option on a line of its own, or two rows of two for four options. Never a ragged 2 + 1, and a word is never broken unless it is wider than the whole control. The track keeps the corner of one row, so a stacked control is a soft rectangle, never an oval. Without JavaScript the cells wrap onto more rows instead.
Reduced motionThe colour change stays; the sliding thumb becomes a fade.
Forced coloursThe track and every option keep a border; the chosen option is Highlight with a 4px frame; the ring is a 4px Highlight outline.
  • Always give the group a name (a legend, or aria-labelledby). The options alone do not say what they choose between.
  • A segmented control applies at once. If the choice needs a confirmation, use a form with a button.
  • If the options swap panels of content, use tabs: different ARIA, different keys.
