Components

Stepper

A stepper is for a small whole number that people nudge up and down: cards per session, guests, reps. Two circle buttons change it by one; a rectangle between them holds the number and can also be typed into. The value is a real <input type="number">, so the arrow keys, forms and validation are the browser's.

When to use

Do

Use a stepper for a bounded whole number, usually under twenty, where each step is meaningful. Set min, max and step, and name the control in the buttons ("Fewer cards"), not "Decrease".

Don't

Use a stepper for money or large numbers (use a plain input or the keypad), or for a value picked by position (a slider).

Examples

Basic

Click, tap or press Enter / Space on a button, or focus the number and use ↑ ↓. The buttons are ordinary circle buttons: they fill and lift on hover and focus and sink when pressed. Hold one to repeat (a tap always works, nothing needs holding). The new value is announced politely, once, after a short pause.

At the limits

At a limit the button becomes aria-disabled: dashed, faint, still focusable. It is not removed and focus is not thrown away, so a keyboard user who presses "More" at the top hears that it is unavailable. A typed value outside the range is pulled back in when the field is left.

Anatomy

  1. Group (.stepper, role="group", aria-labelledby the label): so the two buttons are read with the name of what they change.
  2. Minus and plus: 44 px circle buttons with data-sg-step and a specific aria-label. They point at the input with aria-controls. They stay 44 px at any text size.
  3. Value (.input.stepper__input): an input frame, centred, tabular figures, 20 px. As wide as the longest number it can hold (the digits of min and max, at least two, counted by 32-stepper.js) and exactly as tall as the circles beside it, so the three share one height. The figures grow with the text until they would crowd the box (28 px, 36 px in the large size), then stop, like the amount field's. The native spinner arrows and the mouse-wheel step are removed (they are 10 px targets and change the number by accident).
  4. Row: [−][value][+] is one row, at one height, at every size; the figures grow with the text up to the size their box holds, the circles and the gaps do not.

Accessibility

Stepper keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
TabMinus, then the number, then plus. Each is a stop.
Enter / SpaceOn a button: one step. The new value is announced politely (debounced), because focus stays on the button.
↑ / ↓On the number: native spinbutton stepping.
HoldAfter 450 ms the step repeats and speeds up; release or leave to stop. An enhancement only; tapping always works (WCAG 2.5.1, 2.5.7).
Screen reader"Cards per session, group. Fewer cards, button. Cards per session, spin button, 20. More cards, button." At a limit: "More cards, button, dimmed".
Touch44 px buttons and field, 8 px apart. No gesture is needed.
Zoom / text sizeThe figures grow with the text until they fill 64% of their box (28 px; 36 px large), so at 200% text they keep about 8 px of air instead of touching the frame; the box, the circles and the gaps stay their size, so the three controls keep one row, at one height, in a phone card at 200% text.
Reduced motionNo lift travel; the button fill still fades.
Forced coloursButtons and the field keep their real borders; raised and focused buttons get a 4px Highlight outline; limits use GrayText.
No JavaScriptThe buttons are hidden (@media (scripting: none)) and the browser's own spinner arrows return: a plain number field.

Tokens used

Everything from Input and Button, plus --type-ctl, --text-lg, --text-xl, --chrome-1, --chrome-2, --chrome-3, --chrome-ctl, --chrome-ctl-lg.
