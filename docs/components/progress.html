Components

Progress

Progress answers "how far, and how long?". Every form here writes the value as words as well as length, so nobody has to read a percentage off a bar or tell two hues apart. The same flat pill frame holds all of them, and the meter at the end is the one Card's stat recipe uses.

When to use

Which progress indicator to use
IndicatorUse it forBuilt on
LinearA task with a known length: import, upload, download, a form that saves.<progress value max>
IndeterminateA task that is running but cannot say how long. Swap to linear the moment you can. A short wait is better served by a spinner.<progress> without value
MeterA quantity in a known range: cards learned, storage used, budget spent. It is not a task that finishes.role="meter"
RingOne figure against a goal in a small space: daily cards, a streak, battery.role="progressbar" + SVG
StepsA flow with a fixed number of screens: sign-up, checkout.Text + a segmented bar
Do

Label what is progressing ("Importing deck"), show the number, and add what the number means ("12 of 18 cards") when it helps.

Don't

Fake it. A bar that crawls to 90% and stalls is worse than an honest indeterminate one. And never let colour alone say "done" or "at risk".

Sizes and values

Three heights: 10, 14 and 20px. An empty bar keeps its frame; a full one is solid, so the ends never disappear. The frame is drawn over the fill, so a full bar has no seam inside its round ends. Bars that sit together switch their values together: all beside their labels, or, when one of them does not fit (large text, a narrow column), all under them.

Tone

The fill takes the tone's dark colour and the track its pastel. The words carry the meaning, so a colour-blind reader, the wire palette and forced colours lose nothing. A status under the bar is a .progress__status: an icon and words, with a hidden status word for screen readers. When the status has a fix, it goes beside it in a .progress__foot: Try again below resumes the import.

Motion

Progress motion by setting
SettingWhat you see
DefaultDeterminate bars, meters and rings do not animate: the value is the value. Indeterminate: a segment (40% of the track) slides across in 1.4 s, linearly.
Reduced motionThe segment stops travelling, fills the track, and pulses in opacity over 1.8 s. --move is 0 and every translate is multiplied by it.
Forced coloursFill in Highlight on a Canvas track with a CanvasText frame.

Anatomy

  1. Label (.progress__label): a real <label for>; the accessible name. Meters and rings take aria-label or aria-labelledby.
  2. Value (.progress__value): the number as text, tabular figures, so the layout does not wobble while it counts.
  3. Frame: --bw (2px) in --line (or the card's ink), --radius-pill. The fill is clipped to it, so the left end is round and the value end is flat.
  4. Fill: --card-ink inside a card, --ink otherwise, or --tone-fill with data-tone. Always at least 3:1 against the track.
  5. Track: the surface itself (--card-bg or --paper), or --tone-bg.
  6. Hint (.progress__hint): what the number means ("146 of 214 cards").
  7. Status (.progress__status): what a toned level means, as an icon and words in the tone's status ink, with a hidden status word ("Warning: ") in front. With a fix, it shares a .progress__foot with that one button; the button drops under the words, at the start, when the row is too narrow.

Accessibility

Progress keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
Screen reader<progress> is a progressbar with the label as its name and the value when asked. A meter says "Shapes learned, 5 of 12, meter". Rings say the same as a progressbar. Nothing is announced by itself: milestones go through SG.announce, never every tick (WCAG 4.1.3).
KeyboardNot focusable. There is nothing to operate.
ColourThe value is always text too, and the level of a toned bar is said in words (WCAG 1.4.1). Fill against track is at least 3:1 (1.4.11).
Reduced motionIndeterminate pulses in place instead of sliding.
Zoom / text sizeLabels and values grow with the reader's text size and wrap between words; a value that no longer fits beside its label drops under it, at the start, and the bars, meters and steps that sit together move their values together (measured by 62-progress.js), so the values never zigzag. The bars are chrome: 10, 14 and 20px at every text size. Rings scale with their data-size, so check the centre text at 200%.
Forced coloursHighlight fill, Canvas track, CanvasText frame; ring arc Highlight on a GrayText track.
