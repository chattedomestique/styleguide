Components

Spinner

A spinner says "working" when nothing else can: a save, a refresh, a wait with no shape and no length. It always has a name, it waits 300 ms before it shows, and under reduced motion it pulses instead of turning.

When to use

Do

Put it where the result will land, with words: Saving deck, Loading tasks. Use a skeleton when you know the layout and progress when you know the length.

Don't

Spin for ever. After about ten seconds say something ("Still working. This is taking longer than usual.") and offer a way out. Don't cover the screen for a background refresh.

Examples

Inline, with a visible label

The default. The label is real text, read by everyone. The ring is decorative and aria-hidden.

Loading tasks

Sizes

Four sizes, from text height to a hero loader: 16, 24, 40 and 56px. The ring and its stroke scale together, so the weight looks the same. A ring on its own is chrome: it keeps these sizes at 200% text, where the words around it grow. A ring that leads a visible label is part of that line of words, so it grows with it (1.15 times the label's size, never under 24px), the same mark as the spinner in a busy button.

Label for screen readers only

When the spot is tiny and the meaning obvious, hide the label visually with sr-only: below, a deck that is syncing. The hidden label is still the accessible name. A button that is working needs no spinner element: aria-busy="true" on the button itself draws its own small one, before its label, and ignores taps.

Overlay

Covers a region while it reloads. The overlay is positioned against its parent, so the parent needs position: relative (a card is). The label stays on a solid plate, never directly on the dimmed content. Over a region shorter than 16rem, like this card, the plate covers the whole region: a plate across the middle of a few lines of text would leave cut letters peeking out above and below it. Over a larger region (a list, a screen) the scrim dims everything and the framed plate sits in the middle. Mark the region aria-busy="true" and make what is underneath inert, so a keyboard cannot reach controls nobody can see.

Refreshing

Delay: nothing for the first 300 ms

Add data-delay (to the spinner or the overlay) and it fades in only if the wait outlasts 300 ms. A quick answer then shows no spinner at all, which feels faster than a flash.

Motion

Spinner motion by setting
SettingWhat you see
DefaultThe arc turns once every 800 ms (--anim-spin).
Reduced motion (OS, or the Motion switch)The arc does not turn. It pulses in opacity every 1.4 s. --anim-spin swaps itself, so no component needs a motion media query.
Forced coloursArc in Highlight on a GrayText track. A masked arc keeps its gap, where a border-based spinner turns into a solid circle.

Loading indicators are an essential activity and exempt from WCAG 2.2.2 pause controls, but the words still have to change when the wait turns out to be long.

Anatomy

  1. Ring: a masked track plus a 28% arc (::after), aria-hidden. The arc is currentColor; the track is --line-soft. No <svg> in the markup.
  2. Label: visible (spinner__label) or sr-only. Never absent.
  3. Status role: role="status" on the wrapper.
  4. Overlay (optional): --scrim over the region plus a solid, framed plate that holds the spinner and its label. Over a region under 16rem tall, the plate covers the whole region, framed by the region's own frame.

Accessibility

Spinner keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
Screen readerReads the label ("Loading tasks") through role="status". A live region inserted together with its text is announced by some readers and skipped by others, so for a wait that matters also set aria-busy="true" on the region and announce the result with SG.announce() (WCAG 4.1.3).
KeyboardNot focusable. An overlay does not trap focus: make the content beneath inert, and never cover a Cancel control.
Text contrastThe overlay label sits on a solid paper plate at 7:1, not on the scrim (WCAG 1.4.3).
Reduced motionThe arc pulses instead of turning.
Zoom / text sizeLabels grow with the reader's text size and wrap beside the ring (under it only when their longest word cannot sit beside it); a ring that leads a visible label grows with it. A ring on its own and the plate's insets are chrome and keep their size, so the words keep the room. Browser zoom scales everything.
Forced coloursTrack GrayText, arc Highlight, plate Canvas with a CanvasText frame.
