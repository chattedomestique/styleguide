Components

Switch

A switch turns one thing on or off, and it happens immediately: reminders, sound, dark mode. Underneath it is a real checkbox with role="switch", so Space toggles it, it submits with a form and it is announced as "switch, on". The track is a pill and the thumb a circle, because things you press are round.

When to use

Do

Use a switch for a setting that applies the moment it flips. Name the thing, not the action: Streak reminders, not Turn on reminders. The label must not change when the state does.

Don't

Use a switch for a choice that only counts after a Save button (use a checkbox), for picking one of several options (radios), or for an action (a button).

Examples

Row, with the words On and Off

The label sits at the start, the switch at the end, and the word says the state. The words are aria-hidden: the role already tells a screen reader, and a label that changed with the state ("Reminders off") would be read differently each time. Hover or focus a switch to lift it. The word and the switch are one unit: when the row is too narrow for the label beside them (large text, a narrow column), the label takes the whole first line and the unit drops under it together, at the start.

Switch first

data-layout="start" puts the switch before its label and shrinks the row to its content: a single setting in a toolbar, or beside a heading. A long label wraps beside the switch, and the word On / Off follows the label; when it does not fit after it, it drops under the label, at its start, never to the far end of the first line.

Without the words

data-state="hidden" keeps the words in the DOM but off the screen. The tick cut out of the thumb and its position are still two cues that are not colour.

States

Off, raised (hover), focus, pressed, on and disabled. The forced-state classes (is-hover, is-focus, is-active) are docs-only; real states come from :hover, :focus-visible, :active, :checked and :disabled.

Anatomy

  1. Row (.switch, a <label>): at least 44 px tall; clicking anywhere toggles. It carries --lift.
  2. Input: the real checkbox with role="switch", a 56×44 px transparent box that is the measured target. The input, the track and the thumb stay their size at any text size; the label and the word grow.
  3. Track (input::before): 48×28 px pill, --bw frame. --paper when off, --accent when on; raised by --lift with the hard shadow.
  4. Thumb (input::after): a circle that travels with --fill. --ink when off; --on-accent with a tick cut out of it when on.
  5. Label (.switch__label) and optional hint (.switch__hint).
  6. State words (.switch__state): On / Off, aria-hidden, sharing one cell so the row never changes width. The word and the switch are one unit and drop together.

Accessibility

Switch keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
TabOne stop per switch. The ring wraps the track.
SpaceToggles. (Enter does nothing on a checkbox, as in the APG switch pattern.)
Screen reader"Streak reminders, switch, on". The name never changes with the state; the On / Off words are aria-hidden. A .switch__hint inside the label is read as part of the name ("Streak reminders A nudge at 7:30…"): keep it short, or move it to aria-describedby.
Touch56×44 px input and a full-width label. The change applies at once, so there is no hidden Save.
Zoom / text sizeThe label and the word grow; the switch stays 48×28 with its 44 px target. Below about 8em of room for the label (a phone at 200% text) the word and the switch drop under the label together, aligned to the start, so the pair is never split.
Not colour aloneOn = track fills and the thumb moves and shows a tick and the word says On.
Reduced motionNo lift travel. The thumb does not slide: it lands at the midpoint of the colour change while the track and the word cross-fade.
Forced coloursThe track and thumb are redrawn in CanvasText / Highlight / HighlightText: an outlined track with a dark thumb at the start (off), a Highlight track with a light thumb at the end (on).
