Components

Tag

A tag names something: what kind of thing this is, where it stands, how much it matters. You read it; you do not press it. It is the real version of the wireframe's .wf-pill.

When to use

Do

Keep the words in the tag and make them short: Synced, Due tomorrow, High. Status and priority already carry a symbol; the words are what a screen reader gets.

Don't

Put something you can press in a tag. A filter, a toggle or a removable chip is a Chip. A tag has no hover, no shadow and no pointer, so nobody tries.

Tag or Chip: if tapping it should do something, it is a chip. If it only tells you something, it is a tag.

Examples

Variants

A tag is a pill with a thin frame (1px; the 2px frame is for what you press). The default is paper with ink text. data-tone gives it a tone, which is for rhythm across a screen, never for meaning.

Deck Shapes Colours Numbers to twenty Farm animals Shared

data-variant changes how much fill it carries: soft (the default), solid (the tone's saturated fill) and outline (no fill; it takes the colour of whatever it sits on).

Soft Solid Outline Solid, no tone Bold

Status: a symbol and words

The four status tones are ok, warn, bad and info. A tag in one of them draws its own symbol (check, warning, cross, info) before the words, so the colour is never the only thing that says done or failed. To use a different symbol, put your own <span class="ic"> inside and the built-in one steps aside.

Synced Due tomorrow Sync failed 12 new cards Paid Overdue Expires in 2 days
Not colour alone

Switch the palette to Wire in Appearance: every status still reads, by its symbol and its words. That is the test.

Priority: bars, words and weight

Priority is an order, and an order needs more than a hue. data-priority draws three rising bars (all filled, two, one) in front of the word, and changes the weight of the tag: high is solid, medium gets the heavier 2px frame, low is outline. Add a tone to colour it, or leave it neutral.

High Medium Low High Medium Low

Sizes

Small (20px), medium (24px, the default) and large (32px). A tag is never pressed, so it is allowed to be under 44px. Heights are minimums in rem, and the side padding stops at its 100% size, so at 200% text a short label still holds one line; a long one wraps between words, with its symbol beside the first line.

States

A tag has none, on purpose. No hover, no focus, no pressed, no disabled: those all say "this is a control", and a tag is not one. The cursor stays an arrow (inside a link or a button it takes that element's pointer), it casts no shadow, and it is not a tab stop. It is the same pill in square and soft corners, because a pill is what a tag is. If a tag starts needing a state, it has become a Chip.

In context

On a card, tags sit under the eyebrow or in the foot. On a toned card the default tag stays paper with ink text, so it reads on any tone. In a list row the trail is the place for one.

Anatomy

  1. Pill: --radius-pill, a 1px frame in the text colour (--bw-thin). The frame is a real border, so forced-colours mode still draws the tag.
  2. Symbol (status and priority): a 1.125em mask in the text colour, drawn before the words.
  3. Words: --type-meta at weight 600, sentence case, tabular figures. They wrap between words rather than spill at large text. A label with a separator is one unit: put a no-break space (&nbsp;) on both sides of the dot and between a number and its unit, so "Deck · 20 cards" never splits at the dot.
  4. Fill: --tone-bg (soft), --tone-fill (solid) or none (outline). Neutral is --paper.

API

Tag attributes
AttributeValuesEffect
class="tag"The tag. A <span> or <li>; never a link or a button.
data-tone1…6, ink, ok, warn, bad, infoThe fill and text colours. The four status tones also draw a symbol unless you supply an .ic.
data-emphasisboldA saturated mid-dark fill with near-white text, on the same element as data-tone.
data-variantsolid, outline (default: soft)How much fill. Solid reads --tone-fill and --tone-on-fill; with no tone it is ink and paper.
data-priorityhigh, medium, lowThree bars, plus solid, framed or outline weight. An explicit data-variant still wins.
data-sizesm (20), lg (32) (default 24)Minimum height and text size.

Accessibility

Tag keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
TabNot a tab stop. A tag is text. If it needs to be reachable it is a chip or a link.
Screen readerReads the words in place ("Sync failed"). The symbols are masks with no text, so they are silent and the words do the work. Do not repeat the status in an aria-label.
ColourStatus has a symbol and words; priority has bars, words and weight; tone is rhythm only. Text is 7:1 (--tone-ink on --tone-bg) or 4.5:1 on a solid fill.
TouchNothing to hit. If you put a tag inside a link or a button, the target is that element's.
Zoom / text sizeHeight is a minimum in rem and the words wrap at 200% text and at 320px.
Reduced motionA tag does not move.
Forced coloursThe frame becomes CanvasText, symbols follow the text, and what was solid or medium gets the 2px frame so the weight still shows.

Tokens used

--paper, --ink, --tone-bg, --tone-ink, --tone-fill, --tone-on-fill, --bw, --bw-thin, --radius-pill, --type-meta, --text-xs, --text-sm, --text-md, --space-1, --space-2, --space-3.
