Popover

A popover is a small card that opens next to the control that asked for it: a line of help, a couple of options. It is the native popover attribute, so it sits above everything with no z-index, closes when you click away or press Esc, and never blocks the page behind it.

When to use

Do

Use a popover for help that needs more than a tooltip can hold, or for two or three small controls that belong to one thing: a filter, a sort order, a share link. Give it a title and a Close control.

Don't

Use it for a decision that must be answered first (a dialog), a list of commands (a menu) or anything long (a sheet). Do not put a popover inside a popover, and never hide the only way to do something in one.

Examples

Help

The basic popover: a bar, a few sentences, a Close control. popovertarget opens it with no script; SG keeps aria-expanded on the button in step, moves focus in on open (to autofocus, else the first control, else the card itself, so a screen reader reads it) and gives focus back on close.

Known

A card becomes known after you get it right three times in a row. Miss it once and it goes back to review.

Known cards come back after 4 days.

A few options

Small controls that apply at once need no Save. Native checkboxes keep their keyboard and their accessible state; tab order follows the DOM, and moving focus out of the popover closes it.

Options

With actions

A popover with a field that must be saved gets an action row under a 1px rule (.popover__actions). Cancel is a visible button because light dismiss (clicking outside, Esc) discards what was typed.

Reminder

Placement

data-placement says where it opens relative to the button. It is written in logical terms, so start and end flip in right-to-left text. When there is no room it moves: to the other side first, then below or above with one edge lined up with the button's. On a phone neither side of a button has room for a card, so start and end open below or above it there. A popover never covers its button, and it keeps clear of the screen's edges and of a sticky app bar or dock. Browsers with CSS anchor positioning do it in CSS; the others get the same result from a few lines of script. First where each puts the card, as static previews (data-inline); then press the buttons for the live thing.

Anatomy

  1. Button: any .btn with popovertarget, aria-expanded, and aria-haspopup set to dialog. An icon-only button also carries an aria-label.
  2. Popover: <div class="popover card" popover role="dialog">. The owner's card next to the button: 2px frame, --radius-card, no shadow. 20rem wide by default and never wider than the screen.
  3. Bar (.card__bar, optional): title in uppercase and a Close control. Without a title, name the popover with aria-label.
  4. Body (.card__body): text or a few controls. Scrolls when taller than the screen.
  5. Actions (.popover__actions, optional): pills under a 1px rule, for a popover with a form that needs a Save.

Accessibility

Popover keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
Enter / Space on the buttonOpens the popover and moves focus into it.
TabThrough the popover's controls. Past the last one, focus moves on and the popover closes (it is not a place to park). Nothing behind it is inert, and there is no trap (WCAG 2.1.2).
EscCloses and returns focus to the button.
Focus order (2.4.3)The popover should sit right after its button in the DOM so Tab reaches it naturally.
Screen readerThe button is announced with its collapsed / expanded state (aria-haspopup="dialog"); the popover as "Title, dialog", followed by its text.
TouchThe button and the Close control are 44px. Tap outside to dismiss.
Zoom / text sizeWidth is rem and capped to the screen; the body scrolls when the card is taller than the room beside its button; long words wrap. An action row that no longer fits on one line stacks at full width.
Reduced motionThe 8px settle is multiplied by --move, so only a fade remains.
Forced coloursThe frame becomes CanvasText; the bar gets a real line under it.
  • A popover is for things people can do without. Nothing essential lives only in one.
  • If the popover holds a form that must be submitted, give it an action row and a visible way to cancel; light dismiss discards.
