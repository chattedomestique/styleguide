Button

A button does something on this screen: save, add, send, confirm. If it goes somewhere else, it is a link. Buttons are pills and circles because, in this system, circles and pills act and rectangles hold.

When to use

Do

One primary button per screen or card. Name it with the verb and the thing: Save task, not OK.

Don't

Use a button to navigate (use an <a href>), or put two primary buttons side by side.

Variants

The default is an outline that fills on hover. Primary is filled with the accent at rest. Tone borrows the colours of the tone it sits in: a pastel tone (Share deck), or the ink tone (Study offline).

A button that destroys something

It takes the bad status tone and says so in a second way, an icon and the word Delete, because colour never carries meaning alone: in the wire palette the pink is gone and the trash can is still there. Give it a way out beside it, named just as plainly.

Sizes

Every size has a 44px hit area. The small one is drawn at 36px and extends its tappable area invisibly.

With an icon, and as a circle

Icons are masks: <span class="ic ic--plus" aria-hidden="true">. An icon beside a label grows with the reader's text size, because it is part of the line of words. Put an icon that comes before the words inside .btn__label with them: they are then one line of text, so if the label has to wrap at large text the icon leads its first line and the lines stay centred as one group. An icon after the words (Continue) stays outside the label, at its end. A circle holds one icon and must have an aria-label: a tooltip is not a name. An icon-only circle stays at its drawn size (36, 44 or 56) at any text size, so large text gives its room to the words around it; so does the gap in a row of circles (--cluster-gap: var(--chrome-3), 12px), which keeps the row whole on a phone at 200% text.

States

States come from ARIA and native attributes, so the look cannot disagree with what a screen reader is told. The is-* classes below only force a state for this page. An action button rises and fills on hover and keyboard focus, because filling is what pressing it will do, and sinks back onto the surface while pressed.

Toggle buttons

A toggle (aria-pressed) is a selection control, so its fill means on and nothing else. While it is off, hover and keyboard focus only raise it, and a press tints it; a toggle that filled on hover would look on to anyone passing over it with a mouse. On is filled and its frame doubles: a ring of paper inside the frame, which still reads when the accent is as dark as the frame (and in the wire palette, where there is no colour at all). The name never changes; aria-pressed carries the state.

Anatomy

  1. Frame: --bw (2px) in --line. A real border always exists, so forced-colours mode (which deletes fills and shadows) still shows the control.
  2. Fill: --fill mixes the resting colours toward the accent. An action button: rest 0 (outline), hover, focus and pressed 1. A toggle: 0 while off (a press tints it to 0.15), 1 only when on.
  3. Doubled frame (on): a 2px ring of the resting colour inside the frame, so the frame and the edge of the fill read as two outlines whatever the accent.
  4. Hard shadow: appears with --lift (4px, zero blur) when the button can be pressed; pressing sinks it back. A shadow means "you can press this". On a filled button a 2px gap in the surface colour separates the fill from the shadow, so the two do not fuse into one lump.
  5. Label (.btn__label when there is an icon before the words): sentence case, semibold, verb first, 1–3 words. It wraps between words at large text, centred and balanced, with the icon at the start of its first line.
  6. Icon (optional): 20px mask, decorative when a label is present. It grows with the label; the icon of an icon-only circle does not.
  7. Hit area: an invisible ::after that guarantees 44×44px.

API

Button attributes
AttributeValuesEffect
class="btn"The button. Works on <button> and <a>.
class="btn__label"a <span> holding a leading icon and the wordsOne line of text: a wrapped label keeps the icon at the start of its first line and stays centred. An icon after the words goes outside it.
data-variantprimary, tone (default: secondary)Emphasis. Tone reads --tone-bg/ink/fill/on-fill from data-tone on the button.
data-sizesm (36), lg (56) (default 44)Drawn height. The hit area is always at least 44.
data-shapecircleSquare box for one icon. Needs aria-label.
data-blockFull width. The label is centred, so the side padding is 16px instead of 24.
aria-pressedtrue | falseToggle, a selection control: off rises on hover and focus without filling; on = filled AND a doubled frame, so it never depends on the accent being dark. A primary toggle (play / pause) stays filled and lets its icon say the state. role="radio" with aria-checked (one tool of a group) works the same way.
aria-busy="true"Spinner (a pulse under reduced motion); ignores taps. Announce the result with SG.announce().
disabled / aria-disabled="true"Dashed frame, faint ink, no shadow. Prefer aria-disabled plus a reason in aria-describedby: it stays focusable so people can find out why. SG makes it inert for keyboard too.

Accessibility

Button keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
TabMoves focus to and from the button. The ring (3px, offset 3px) plus a paper halo stays visible on every background.
Enter / SpaceActivates. Native <button> gives this for free; never use a <div>.
Screen reader"Label, button". Toggles add pressed / not pressed (the name stays the same); busy adds busy; aria-disabled adds unavailable.
Touch44×44px minimum hit area. No gesture required.
Zoom / text sizeThe label, and an icon beside it, grow with the text; the side padding, the icon gap and icon-only circles stop at their 100% size, so the words get the room. Labels wrap between words rather than clip at 200% text (in .btn__label the icon leads the first line and the lines stay centred), a row of buttons wraps the whole button before it squeezes a label, and a tall wrapped button stays a rounded rectangle (the pill radius is half of one line, not an oval).
Reduced motionNo lift travel; the fill change stays. The spinner pulses instead of rotating.
Forced coloursThe frame becomes ButtonText; raised and focus get a 4px Highlight outline; on gets a 4px frame.
