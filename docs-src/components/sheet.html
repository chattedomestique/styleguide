Sheet

A sheet slides up from the bottom edge to show more about one thing without leaving the screen: a deck's cards, a filter form, a queue. On a wide screen it becomes a side panel. It is a native <dialog> wearing the card's frame, so focus, Esc and the inert page come from the browser, and its handle is a real button.

When to use

Do

Use a sheet for content or a short task that belongs to the screen underneath: the detail of a tapped item, a filter form, a picker. Give it a title and a visible Close control, and let the content scroll inside.

Don't

Rely on a swipe to close or resize it. Do not use a sheet for a yes/no question (that is a dialog), for a hint (a popover) or for moving between screens.

Every gesture has a button

Close: the Close control, Esc, a tap on the scrim, or (optionally) a drag down. Resize: the handle is a button, or a drag up. Nothing needs a swipe (WCAG 2.5.7).

Examples

Detail sheet, half and full height

A deck's detail: one big number and the cards behind it. It opens at about half height with the content scrolling inside. The handle is a button named Full height: tap it, press Enter or Space, or use ArrowUp / ArrowDown on it. Dragging it is an extra. The body holds links, so it needs no extra tab stop.

Side panel on wide screens

From 48em the same markup docks to the inline-end edge at the full height of the screen and slides in from that side. There is no half or full any more, so the handle is hidden and nothing drags. Open any sheet above in a window 768px wide or more to see it. In right-to-left text it docks to the left. Below is that panel as a static pane (data-inline data-side), the way a split view in an app would use it: the frame is on the side that faces the page, and there is no handle.

The handle, in each state

A pill, so it acts, but it is a selection control, so the solid fill means one thing: Full height is on. At rest it is an outline. Hover and keyboard focus raise the outline (its hard shadow says "you can press this"), a press sinks it and tints it, and on is filled and its frame doubles (a second cue that does not depend on the accent being dark). Hovering an "on" handle raises it, with a gap of paper between the fill and its shadow. The is-* classes only force a state for this page.

Anatomy

  1. Sheet: <dialog class="sheet card"> (modal) or <section class="sheet card" popover="manual"> (persistent). The owner's card, docked to the bottom: 2px frame on three sides, top corners --radius-card, at most 36rem wide and centred above that. No shadow.
  2. Handle (.sheet__handle): a 56×28px button at the top. Its 48×16px pill is drawn by ::before, 4px in from each side so the raised pill and its hard shadow stay inside the box and the focus ring, round the box, never touches them; the hit area reaches 44px. It is chrome: it stays the same size at 200% text. aria-pressed, a label that never changes; only "on" is filled.
  3. Bar (.card__bar): the title in uppercase (.card__label, a heading) and the Close control (.card__ctl, 44px). aria-labelledby points at the heading.
  4. Body (.card__body): the only part that scrolls. Its bottom padding includes the home-indicator inset. A .card__list inside it bleeds edge to edge.
  5. Actions (.sheet__actions, optional): pills pinned below the body, primary last. One right-aligned row while they all fit; otherwise every pill is full width, one per line, in the same order (measured by 61-sheet.js), so they never wrap into a ragged one-and-one.
  6. Scrim: --scrim, modal sheets only.

Accessibility

Sheet keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
Tab / Shift+TabModal: cycles inside the sheet; the page is inert (never a trap: Close and Esc always leave, WCAG 2.1.2). Non-modal: moves through the page as usual; the panel sits right after its button in the DOM (2.4.3).
EscCloses and returns focus to the button that opened it. On a non-modal sheet, only while focus is inside it.
Enter / SpaceOn Close: closes. On the handle: toggles full height.
ArrowUp / ArrowDownOn the handle: full height / half height.
Screen reader"Title, dialog". The handle is "Full height, toggle button, pressed / not pressed". Close is "Close, button".
TouchClose and the handle are 44px targets. Tap the scrim to close, tap the handle to resize. Dragging is optional (2.5.7).
ScrollingA body that holds nothing focusable becomes a named tab stop so the keyboard can scroll it. It never scrolls the page behind (overscroll-behavior: contain).
Safe areasThe sheet is inset by the left and right insets; the body and actions pad the bottom inset; a side panel's bar pads the top inset. Landscape phones keep their side insets.
Zoom / text sizeHeights use dvh with the top inset removed. The words grow with the reader's text size; the handle, the bar's height and the insets of the actions stop at their 100% size. At 200% text the body simply scrolls, and actions that no longer fit on one row stack at full width, one width and one height.
Reduced motionNo travel: the sheet fades in and out. Resizing still eases, in 180ms. A drag still follows the finger, and releasing to dismiss fades instead of sliding.
Forced coloursThe card's frame becomes CanvasText; the handle keeps a ButtonText frame, raised gets a 4px Highlight outline and on gets a 4px frame; the bar gets a real line.
  • The Close control is always visible. The reference design's grabber measured 1.6:1 and was the only control: the pattern this page exists to replace.
  • A sheet's title names it: aria-labelledby pointing at the visible heading.
  • The half/full change animates height (layout, once per tap). It is the one place this system moves something other than transform and opacity, because a detent has no fade equivalent.
