Slider

Choose a value on a range by position: volume, a study goal, a budget. It is the browser's own <input type="range">, so every key works, plus a readout people can read and buttons people can tap. The track is a rectangle that holds; the thumb is a circle that acts.

When to use

Do

Use a slider when the relative position matters more than the exact number and the range is bounded. Always show the value with its unit, and add the − / + buttons when precision matters or dragging is hard.

Don't

Use a slider for an exact amount, a wide range (0 to 10,000) or a small count. Drag is never the only way to change a value (WCAG 2.5.7).

Slider, stepper or number field?

Choosing between a slider, a stepper and a number input
UseWhenExamples
SliderA bounded range where "about here" is fine, or the feedback is live.Volume, brightness, filter strength, budget level
StepperA small whole number changed by one at a time.Cards per session, guests, servings, reps
Number inputAn exact or large number the person knows. Pair it with a slider if they also want to explore.Amount, age, weight, year

Examples

With nudge buttons

The readout and the spoken value update as you drag or press keys. The − / + buttons step by one step; at the ends they become aria-disabled (they keep focus) and every press is announced ("Volume: 45%"). Try ← →, PageUp, PageDown, Home, End. The thumb is an outline until you hover or focus it, then it fills and lifts; dragging sinks it.

Tick marks and currency

data-ticks="5" draws five marks, both ends included. They are decoration: the slider already exposes its minimum and maximum. data-format="currency" uses Intl.NumberFormat, so the symbol and grouping follow the page language; set data-currency for other currencies.

Minutes, without buttons

The smallest form: label, readout, slider. Units come from data-format="minutes": "25 min" on screen, "25 minutes" to a screen reader. Leave the buttons out only when the slider is a convenience and the exact value does not matter, since they are what meets WCAG 2.5.7 without a drag.

Signed values

Negative numbers use the true minus sign (U+2212) on screen. data-format="plain" is a bare number; data-format="unit" with data-unit="celsius" (or kilometer, percent…) uses any Intl unit.

A fine dial: filter intensity

Anatomy

  1. Label (.slider__label): a real <label for>, always visible.
  2. Readout (<output class="slider__value">): the value with its unit, tabular numerals. aria-live="off" so it does not repeat what the slider already says.
  3. Nudge buttons (optional, data-sg-nudge): ordinary 36 px circle buttons with 44 px hit areas and 12 px gaps. The row is [−] track [+] at every size: the track takes the room that is left.
  4. Rail: 12 px, a --bw frame in --line, --radius-ctl. The filled part is --accent, painted to the thumb's centre from --p (0 to 1) that 31-slider.js sets.
  5. Thumb: a 28 px circle, an outline at rest that fills and lifts on hover and focus and sinks while dragging. The input is 44 px tall, so the touch target is 44.
  6. Ticks (optional, data-ticks) and scale (.slider__scale): marks and minimum / maximum captions, aria-hidden.

Accessibility

Slider keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
← ↓ / → ↑Decrease / increase by one step. Native. In right-to-left pages the direction flips.
PageDown / PageUpLarger step (a tenth of the range, at least one step). Native.
Home / EndMinimum / maximum. Native.
Nudge buttonsThe single-pointer alternative to dragging (WCAG 2.5.7). Enter or Space steps; the new value is announced politely because focus stays on the button.
Screen reader"Volume, slider, 40 percent". aria-valuetext carries the unit; the readout is not announced twice.
TouchDrag the thumb or tap the track. The input is 44 px tall; the thumb is 28 px.
Zoom / text sizeThe label, the readout and the captions grow with the text; the thumb, the rail, the ticks, the 44 px input and the nudge circles stay their size, so [−] track [+] keeps one row (about 260 px of track on a 390 px phone at 200% text). A readout that no longer fits beside its label drops under it, at the start.
FocusThe ring wraps the thumb (with its paper halo).
Reduced motionNo lift travel; the thumb's fill still fades.
Forced coloursRail frame, fill (Highlight) and thumb (ButtonFace / ButtonText) use system colours; a focused thumb gets a 4px Highlight outline.
No JavaScriptThe slider, its label, its keys and its value all work. The fill, the readout, the ticks and the nudge buttons need script, so they are hidden (@media (scripting: none)) rather than left dead or stale.
