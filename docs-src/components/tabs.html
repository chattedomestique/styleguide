Tabs

Tabs show one panel of related content at a time. All the panels belong to the same screen; the tabs only decide which one is in view.

When to use

Do

Use two to six sibling views of one thing: Cards | Progress, Overview | Activity | Settings. One or two words each, the most useful view first.

Don't

Use tabs to move around the app (that is the dock), to set a value (that is a segmented control), or to walk through steps in order. Don't nest tabs inside tabs.

Examples

Pill (default)

A row of pills. The chosen tab is filled and its frame doubles (a 2px ring in the surface colour just inside the border, so it stays the size of the tabs beside it), so it never depends on the accent being dark. The fill is kept for the chosen tab: hover and keyboard focus raise a tab with the same hard shadow a button has, and a press tints it, but only the chosen one is solid. Click, or Tab in and use the arrow keys. When large text or a narrow screen leaves no room for every tab, the row scrolls and runs to the edge of the panel; the tab cut at that edge says there is more, and it is cut near its middle on purpose (the tabs change width a little, all by the same amount, until it is), never a sliver and never just its end. A row that misses by a few pixels is tightened to fit instead.

Sizes and full width

data-size="sm" draws the tab at 36px; its hit area stays 44px. data-block shares the row out equally, which suits two or three tabs on a phone.

With an action beside the tabs

An action that is not a tab sits outside the tablist, in the bar. It is a real button with its own name. Everything in one bar is one height: these tabs are small, so the action and the scroll buttons are small too (data-size="sm": 36px drawn, 44px target), and at large text they all grow with the words together. When large text leaves no room for every tab, the list pages and its scroll buttons appear on either side of it (see below), so the action stays where it is and the hidden tabs are one press away.

When there are too many to fit

The tablist scrolls sideways. Write the two scroll buttons, tabs__scroll, hidden, on either side of the list: the script shows them while the list overflows and dims the one that cannot go further (aria-disabled, so the row never jumps). Beside them the tabs are cut into pages: each page is the run of tabs that fits whole, and the next page starts just past the edge, so at every page every tab in view is whole and nothing is sliced beside a button. The tabs keep their own widths; what a page does not fill stays empty, and the forward button says there is more. A press goes one page, a swipe snaps to one, and moving focus with the arrow keys brings the focused tab's page into view. The buttons are for a pointer that cannot swipe; keyboard users have the arrow keys, so the buttons are not tab stops. Nothing is hidden behind a menu. Without the buttons a row simply scrolls (swipe, trackpad, arrow keys): let it run to the edge of the screen or panel, never stop short of a frame, and the script changes its tabs' widths a little, all by the same amount, until the tab cut at that edge shows about half of itself at rest, so the cut reads as "more" and never as a stray sliver or a sliced end (a row that misses by a few pixels is tightened to fit).

States

The look follows aria-selected and native attributes, so it cannot disagree with what a screen reader is told. The solid fill is kept for the chosen tab: hover and focus raise, a press tints. The is-* classes only force a state for this page. data-overflow="wrap" lets the tabs wrap onto a second line instead of scrolling; use it for a specimen like this one, not for a real tab row, where a second line of tabs reads as a second set.

Anatomy

  1. Bar (.tabs__bar): the tablist and, optionally, its two scroll buttons and one action that is not a tab. One height for all of them: 44px (36 small) at normal text; at large text the label plus 8px above and below, and the circles grow with it. In the underline variant it carries the 1px divider.
  2. Tablist (.tabs__list, role="tablist"): scrolls sideways when the tabs overflow. 8px of padding keeps rings and shadows from being clipped; a matching negative margin keeps alignment.
  3. Scroll buttons (.tabs__scroll, optional): circle buttons with a chevron, the height of the tabs beside them (one row, one height), one each side of the list, shown only while it overflows; the one at an end is aria-disabled. While they show, the tabs page: every tab in view is whole.
  4. Tab (.tabs__tab, role="tab"): a pill with a 2px frame, 44px tall (36px with data-size="sm"), 16px of side padding (12px small), hit area always 44px. Label in sentence case, semibold; an optional icon before it.
  5. Chosen cue: pill is filled and its frame doubles inside (a 2px ring in the surface colour between the border and the fill); underline is a 2px bar and full ink. Never colour alone. The fill means chosen and nothing else.
  6. Panel (.tabs__panel, role="tabpanel"): one visible at a time, tabindex="0" so a keyboard user can reach it when it holds nothing focusable. Fades in; under reduced motion it is a plain fade with no travel.

Accessibility

The WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices "Tabs" pattern, with automatic activation by default.

Tabs keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
TabEnters the tablist on the chosen tab (the only tab stop in the list), then moves into the open panel. Shift+Tab comes back.
Left / RightPrevious / next tab, wrapping, skipping disabled ones; selects it (automatic) or only moves focus (manual). Mirrored in right-to-left layouts.
Up / DownThe same in a vertical tablist.
Home / EndFirst / last enabled tab.
Enter / SpaceSelect the focused tab (manual activation).
Screen reader"Overview, tab, selected, 1 of 3", inside a tablist named by aria-label; the panel is named by its tab (aria-labelledby). Inactive panels are hidden, so they are not read.
Touch and pointer44×44px hit area on every tab. A tablist that overflows scrolls by touch (a swipe snaps to a page), and its scroll buttons page it for a pointer that cannot swipe (WCAG 2.5.1); no gesture is required to reach a tab.
Zoom / text sizeThe words grow with the text; the side padding, the insets, the gaps and the 44px targets do not, and the whole bar keeps one height. At 200% text and at 320px a row that no longer fits pages beside its scroll buttons instead of clipping or wrapping, every tab in view whole, and the focused tab's page is brought into view. A label in a vertical list wraps at its spaces and keeps the one-line corner (a soft rectangle, never an oval).
Reduced motionNo lift travel and no panel travel; the fill change and a short fade stay. Paging and scrolling a tab into view are instant.
Forced coloursFrames become ButtonText; raised and focused tabs get a 4px Highlight outline; the chosen pill gets a 4px frame, the chosen underline a 4px Highlight bar.
  • Do not put aria-current on a tab and do not use aria-selected on a link: tabs swap panels in place, links go somewhere.
  • Keep labels stable: the text of a tab never changes with its state.
  • If a panel can be long, give it a heading inside; the tab name is not a heading.
