Segmented control

A segmented control sets one value out of a few that are equally likely: Weekly | Monthly, Bank account | Transactions. It is a pill track with the chosen option filled in.

When to use

Do

Use two to four options with short labels, and one always chosen. It changes how the content beneath it is shown, or a setting, straight away.

Don't

Use it to navigate (links in a dock), to swap panels of different content (tabs), for five or more options, or for a choice that can be empty (chips).

Examples

Basic

Native radio inputs in a <fieldset> with a <legend>. There is no script: Tab enters the group once, on the chosen option; Left and Right move and choose; the value posts with a form. The legend can be visually hidden when the control is labelled by the text next to it.

Show

Three and four options, with icons

Options share the width equally, so a longer label never pushes the others around and choosing one never reflows the row. Past four options, use a menu or chips. An icon goes before the word, never instead of it, and is left out at four options, where there is no room for both.

Sliding thumb

data-indicator="slide" replaces the per-option fill with one thumb that glides to the chosen option. Under reduced motion the thumb stays out of the way and each option fades its own fill in: a fade, never a jump cut.

Theme

Inside a tonal card

data-variant="tone" reads the tone of data-tone on the control itself, so it matches the card it sits in. This is the weekly / monthly toggle of a stat card.

Anatomy

  1. Track (.segmented): a pill with a 2px --line frame, filled with --paper-2, 4px of padding. A <fieldset> with a <legend>, or a role="radiogroup" with a visible label.
  2. Option (.segmented__opt): a <label> holding a native radio. Equal width.
  3. Radio: invisible, covering the option and stretching to 44px tall. All keyboard and form behaviour is the browser's.
  4. Label (.segmented__label): the visible cell. Its fill follows --fill (0 empty, 1 chosen). Optional icon, then the word.
  5. Chosen cue: a filled pill with a frame of its own inside the track (a second ring). The weight does not change, so the row cannot reflow. Never colour alone.
  6. Thumb (optional, data-indicator="slide"): one pill that glides between options.

Accessibility

A native radio group (APG Radio Group), so the browser supplies the behaviour below.

Segmented control keyboard and screen reader behaviour
InteractionBehaviour
Tab / Shift+TabEnters the group once, on the chosen option, and leaves on the next press.
Left / Right (and Up / Down)Move to the previous / next option and choose it, wrapping; unavailable options are skipped.
SpaceChooses the focused option (when none is chosen).
Screen reader"Show, group. Bank account, radio button, 1 of 2, selected." The legend names the group.
TouchEach option is at least 44×44px, even in the small size, and the whole cell taps, not only the word.
Zoom / text sizeAt 200% text the options wrap inside their cell; nothing clips or scrolls sideways at 320px.
Reduced motionThe colour change stays; the sliding thumb becomes a fade.
Forced coloursThe track and every option keep a border; the chosen option is Highlight with a 4px frame; the ring is a 4px Highlight outline.
  • Always give the group a name (a legend, or aria-labelledby). The options alone do not say what they choose between.
  • A segmented control applies at once. If the choice needs a confirmation, use a form with a button.
  • If the options swap panels of content, use tabs: different ARIA, different keys.
