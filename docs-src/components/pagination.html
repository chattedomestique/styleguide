Pagination

Pagination splits a long list into pages and lets people move between them. It says which page you are on, how many there are, and never hides the way forward or back.

When to use

Do

Use it for a list people scan and return to, where a page is a stable thing: search results, a library of decks, a ledger. Make every page a real link, so Back and a shared URL work.

Don't

Use it to move between sections of an app (the dock), up a hierarchy (breadcrumb) or through steps. For a feed that just keeps going, a "Load more" button is simpler than pages nobody can jump to.

Examples

A working pager

Previous, the first page, a window of pages around the current one, the last page, Next. Gaps are written … and are not controls. The page you are on is filled and its frame doubles (a 2px ring in the surface colour just inside the border, so it is the size of the numbers beside it), and it is marked aria-current="page"; another number only lifts when you point at it. The row is always one row: on a wide screen you see all of it, on a phone the pager drops the words of Previous and Next, then keeps only the first, current and last page (see the short form below). This one re-renders on click so you can walk through all 12; in an app each number is a link to a URL and the browser does the work.

    Narrow: the short form

    The pager measures its own width, not the screen's, and shows the richest form that fits on one row: the full row; then Previous and Next as 44px circles that keep their words for assistive technology; then only the first, current and last page; then one line, Page 5 of 12, between the two circles: three big targets on any phone. The short form stays one row at every text size: the line wraps between the circles, it never drops under them. The box below starts at 16rem on every screen; where there is room, drag its corner wider to watch the numbers come back, a step at a time.

    As a month switcher

    data-variant="switcher" keeps the short form at any width, for a previous / next pair around one value that is not a page number: a month, a week, a deck. The value is the biggest thing in the row, and at large text it wraps between the two circles. It changes the view in place, so the controls are buttons, and the value is a polite live region: a screen reader hears "February 2026" when you press Next. The buttons keep their names current ("Previous month, January").

    Anatomy

    1. Landmark (<nav class="pagination" aria-label="… pages">): a labelled navigation region; its own width decides the form.
    2. Previous / Next (pagination__prev, pagination__next): buttons as pills with an arrow, rel="prev" / rel="next"; 44px circles when the row is short of room. Their words are in pagination__label.
    3. Page (pagination__page): a circle button with a tabular number, which grows with the text. Its aria-label is "Page N".
    4. Current page: aria-current="page"; filled, its frame doubled inside (a 2px ring in the surface colour within the border, so it is the size of the numbers beside it). The only filled number.
    5. Gap (pagination__gap): …, aria-hidden, not a control.
    6. Status (pagination__status): "Page 5 of 12", shown only in the short form. In the switcher it is the value itself.

    Pagination classes and attributes
    NameValuesEffect
    class="pagination"on a <nav aria-label>The landmark. src/js/47-pagination.js measures it and writes the form that fits to data-fit.
    data-fitfull, compact, fewer, short (set by script)Words on Previous / Next and every number; circles for Previous / Next; only the first, current and last page; the status line. Read it, do not write it. Without JS, under 38rem of its own width, the short form.

    pagination__labela span inside Previous / NextThe word; visually hidden but kept in the name in the short form.
    pagination__gap, pagination__statuslist itemsThe gap between page numbers; the "Page N of M" line.
    data-variant="switcher"Always the short form, with the status as a large value between the two buttons. For months, weeks, decks.
    aria-current="page"on the current page's linkFilled + its frame doubled inside. Exactly one.
    aria-disabled="true"on a Previous / Next <button>Dashed and faint; inert; explain why in aria-describedby.

    Accessibility

    Pagination keyboard and screen reader behaviour
    InteractionBehaviour
    Tab / Shift+TabEvery control is a tab stop, in visual order: Previous, the numbers, Next. An unavailable Previous/Next is still reachable.
    EnterFollows the link.
    Screen reader"Decks pages, navigation; list, 9 items; Previous, link; Page 4, link; Page 5, current page, link; …; Next, link". In the short form: "Previous, link; Page 5 of 12; Next, link". An unavailable control reads "Previous, button, unavailable. You are on the first page".
    Touch44×44px circles with 8px between them. In the short form, three large targets.
    Zoom / text sizeOne row at every size; nothing wraps, nothing runs out of its frame or scrolls sideways. The page numbers grow with the text; Previous and Next as circles do not. As the room shrinks (a phone, 200% text) the pager steps down: circles for Previous and Next, then fewer numbers, then the short form, where the status line may wrap onto two lines between the circles.
    Right-to-leftThe order mirrors and the Previous / Next arrows flip to point the right way.
    Reduced motionButtons raise without travel.
    Forced coloursThe buttons keep their borders; the current page gets a 4px frame.
    • When a page change does not reload (a single-page app), move focus to the heading of the results and announce the new position with SG.announce(), as the live pager above does. Otherwise a screen-reader user hears nothing happen.
    • Name the nav for what it pages ("Decks pages"), so two pagers on one page are distinguishable.
    • Number formatting follows the locale (Intl.NumberFormat); page numbers are tabular so the row does not jitter.

    Icons. arrow-left and arrow-right come from dist/icons.css; the main bundle ships only plus, minus, close, check and arrow. Link icons.css in your app, or define the masks yourself: an .ic with no mask paints a solid square.

    Tokens used

    Everything is a button, so it reads the button's tokens; Pagination adds --fill, --bw, --bw-heavy, --line, --type-ctl, --type-title, --chrome-ctl, --chrome-ic-sm, --chrome-2 to --chrome-4, --space-4 and the size container pagination (38rem for the short form without JS).
