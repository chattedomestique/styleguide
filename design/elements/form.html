Forms

Fields together, and what happens when they're wrong. One column, labels above, and errors only once you send it: then a summary at the top that takes you to each one.

01

Anatomy

Form owns the layout, the optional mark, the summary and the submit row. Every field in it is its own element: Field, Checkbox, Radio, Select, Stepper.

    "(optional)" inside the label, so it is heard with the name. Required fields carry no mark and use required.

    A <fieldset> and a <legend> for choices that answer one question. Its error belongs to the whole group.

  5. 5
    Submit row.form__actions

    Cancel (quiet), then the primary. Wide: in a row, primary last. Narrow: full width, primary on top.

    04

    Usage

    1. Do

      Ask only what you need, in the order people think of it. Mark the optional fields. Write each error as the fix, and name the button for what it does: "Create deck", not "Submit".

    2. Don't

      Don't check while someone types. Don't disable the button until the form is valid: let them send it and say what's missing. Don't put a switch in a form with Save.

    3. Installed PWA

      Full-width buttons, primary on top, within reach of a thumb. Controls keep 6rem of room above them when they scroll into view, so the label and the error aren't hidden under a bar.

    4. Mac and SwiftUI

      Return sends; the primary is last in the row. In SwiftUI, a VStack of our fields, labels above, rather than a Form, whose styles put labels beside fields. The summary takes VoiceOver with @AccessibilityFocusState.
