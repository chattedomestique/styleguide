Select

One choice from a long list. We draw the closed control, in Field's square frame; the phone and the Mac open their own picker, because they know it best.

01

Anatomy

Field's frame, label, help and error, in Field's order, around a native <select> and the down glyph. Only the select and the glyph are new.

02

Variants

Three, and they differ only in what sits above the select. Single choice, always: a list you pick several from is a group of checkboxes.

2.1

Default

A label and the select.

.field .field__label .select .select__input .select__icon

Languages are listed in their own words, each option with its lang, so a screen reader says "Français" in French. autocomplete="off": this is the deck's language, not yours.

2.2

With help

When the choice needs a word of explanation.

.field__help

The help goes between the label and the select, tied with aria-describedby. Deck colours are named, never swatches: the palette is monochrome, so a name is what tells them apart.

Fills the deck's card in your decks.

2.3

Invalid

Nothing chosen where something must be.

.field__error aria-invalid

Only where there is no sensible default: an empty first option ("Choose a language") and required. Form checks on submit; then the frame doubles and the error above says how to fix it.

Cards are read aloud in this language.

03

States

Field's states, minus the ones a select can't have: it always shows a value, and it has no read-only. It holds a value, so it never lifts.

Rest. A 2px square frame on paper, and the down glyph.

Hover. The ground turns to canvas, as a field's does. Pointers only.

Focus. A 3px ring, 3px out. Space, Enter or the arrows open the picker.

Pressed. Canvas, as on hover, so a tap answers while the picker opens.

Pick a colour for the deck

Invalid. The frame doubles inwards, and the fix sits above it. No red.

Built-in decks keep their language. Duplicate the deck to change it.

Disabled. Dashed and faint, out of the tab order, and the help says why.

The .is-* classes force a state for this page and for tests. They are not API: real states come from :hover (only where a pointer hovers), :focus-visible, :active, aria-invalid and disabled.

04

Usage

  1. Do

    Use it for a long list of known options: languages, colours. Pick a sensible default, so the select is never empty. Name every option in words.

  2. Don't

    Don't use it for two to four options that fit on screen: show them, as a segmented control or a radio group. Don't use it to navigate, or for several choices at once.

  3. Installed PWA

    The phone opens its own picker: a wheel on iOS, a list on Android. 16px text, or iOS zooms. appearance: none takes away iOS's grey gradient.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    The Mac opens its own menu, with type-ahead. In SwiftUI, a Picker with .pickerStyle(.menu), our label above it and its own hidden.
