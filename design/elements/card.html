  1. 1
    Frame.card

    2px, --card-line. Clips its contents, and is the container that cqi and @container card measure against.

  2. 2
    Bar.card__bar

    The frame made thicker: ink fill, a label at the start, one control at the end. The control is a 44px target around a 20px box.

  3. 3
    Media.card__media

    Edge to edge, 4:3 unless --ratio says otherwise. Closed off by a 2px rule, never a shadow.

  4. 4
    Body.card__body

    Where the padding lives: clamp(1rem, 5cqi, 1.5rem), so a narrow card is snug and a wide one breathes.

  5. 5
    Eyebrow.card__eyebrow

    14px, soft ink. Says what kind of thing this is.

  6. 6
    Title.card__title

    Weight 500, 22 to 32px depending on the card's width. Balanced, so it never leaves one word on its own line.

  7. 7
    Text.card__text

    16px on a 1.5 line, 40 characters wide at most.

  8. 8
    Foot.card__foot

    A 1px rule, then the small print. Sticks to the bottom, so cards in a row end on one line. A link in it gets the full 44px target.

  9. 9
    Action.card__action

    A 44px circle. Round means it does something, so it is always a real button. In a ghost card it marks the card, which is the button.

The default. A title, a line of support, somewhere to go.

2.6

Link

The whole card goes somewhere.

.card--link .card__link

One anchor, in the title, stretched over the card with ::after. One tab stop, one accessible name, and the whole card is the target. The card wears the focus ring, not the anchor. Anything else clickable inside must sit above it. The title's underline on hover and focus is the cue; no arrow needed.

2.8

Study

The flashcard itself. 5:7, one subject, one hint.

.card--study .card__stage .card__answer .card__flip .card__turn .card__face

One face, or both and the Flip button. The live card turns over on a native checkbox drawn as Button's icon-only circle, "Show the answer", so it works without script: Space or a click, and the state is announced. Perspective on the card, both faces hidden from behind, --ease-flip over --dur-move. The face turned away leaves the accessibility tree and the Tab order halfway through, when it's edge-on. Under reduced motion the faces crossfade in place. The card never grows taller than the screen: inside a Frame, --chrome is measured for you; outside one, set it to the height of the bars. It never gets narrower than 8rem.

The .is-* classes force a state for this page and for tests. They are not API: real states come from :hover (only where a pointer hovers), :focus-visible, :active, aria-current on a card's link, aria-selected on a card that is an option in a listbox, and the .card--disabled modifier.

Tiled grid

No gaps. The frames are shared.

4.3

Container sizing

A card answers to its own width, not the window.

cqi @container card

The same two cards at three widths (each one capped by the stage on a small screen). Padding, titles and the big figure scale with the card; the row's value drops under its title at 18rem and below. Nothing here looks at the viewport.
