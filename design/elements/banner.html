Banner

News about the whole screen, at the top of it: you're offline, a new version is ready, your decks didn't load. A glyph and words, never colour, and it's there for as long as it's true.

01

Anatomy

A band across the top of the scroll region, under the top bar: a glyph, the words, and at most one action. It is part of the content, so it scrolls away with it.

02

Variants

Three, told apart by their glyph and words. Each is shown under a top bar, where it lives; on a screen it is the first thing in the scroll region.

2.1

Offline

No connection. Everything still works, from this device.

.banner ic--offline role="status"

Up for as long as it's true, and no close: closing it wouldn't make it untrue. No action either, because there's nothing to do. Wave 5's service worker raises it, and takes it down when the connection is back.

2.2

Update ready

A new version has downloaded and is waiting.

.banner ic--refresh .banner__action role="status"

Reload swaps it in. It can wait for the end of a session, so it never interrupts: it sits at the top and scrolls away. An installed app has no reload button of its own, so this is the way to the new version short of closing the app.

2.3

Failure

Something about the whole screen didn't work.

.banner ic--warning .banner__action role="status"

Status while the screen still shows something you can use (the decks from the last time); role="alert" only when it blocks (States, below). When there is nothing to show at all, the failed card in the content says it instead: once, where the decks would be.

03

States

The band doesn't press or lift; its action is Button's. What changes is how loudly it speaks and how it fits. Forced here with .is-* on the button.

A new version is ready.

Rest. Appears in place, no motion, even at gate 4: a band that pushes the content down is layout, and motion moves only transform and opacity. A status: read out once, politely, when it goes up.

A new version is ready.

Action, hover. Button's: the pill fills and lifts. Pointers only.

A new version is ready.

Action, focus. The hover look and the ring, which fits inside the band's 8px.

A new version is ready.

Action, pressed. Down, filled, the inner ring. Then the app reloads.

You're offline. Changes are saved on this device.

Narrow, or large text. The words wrap beside the glyph; an action moves under them, at the end. The band grows taller, never wider.

Couldn't load your decks.

Blocking. The same band with role="alert": read out at once, because nothing on the screen works until it does. The only assertive banner.

Waiting: <div class="banner" role="status"></div>, with nothing inside, takes no room and draws nothing; filling it raises it and is what's read out. In forced colours the rule is CanvasText and the glyph is drawn in it too; the band's paper becomes the system's background, so the rule is what sets it off. The .is-* classes force a state for this page and for tests; they are not API.

04

Usage

  1. Do

    Put it first in the scroll region. Say what's true and what it means for you, offer one way on, and take it down the moment it stops being true. One at a time: the one that changes what you can do now (a blocking failure, then offline, then an update).

  2. Don't

    Don't float it, pin it or lay it over content; don't give the offline banner a close; don't use one for "it's done" (that's a Toast) or for a question (that's a Dialog). No tinted fill to say what kind: the glyph and the words say it.

  3. Installed PWA

    Wave 5's service worker raises offline and update ready for real. Keep the band in the markup, empty, and fill it: a live region speaks when it changes, not when it arrives. Empty, it takes no room and draws nothing.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    The same band at the top of the window's content, under the toolbar, scrolling with it: in SwiftUI, the first row of the ScrollView, not a safeAreaInset, which would pin it.
