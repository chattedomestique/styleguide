Switch

A setting that takes effect at once. A pill and a circle, because you act on it. On moves the knob to the end, fills the track and says "On", so it never needs colour.

01

Anatomy

The settings row: the words lead, the switch trails, and the whole row is the label, so a tap anywhere on it flips the switch.

1

At 6pm, if you haven't studied today.

  1. 1
    Row.switch__label

    The whole row is a <label>, at least 44px tall: the target. The words and the switch share a line, and the switch drops below when they can't.

  2. 2
    Words.switch__text

    The setting's name, 16px. It is the switch's accessible name, and it never changes.

  3. 3
    State.switch__state

    "On" or "Off", in words, beside the track, never in it. Both words share one cell, so the track never shifts. aria-hidden: the switch already says it.

  4. 4
    Track and knob.switch__input

    A checkbox with role="switch", drawn: a 52 by 28 pill with a 2px frame, and a 20px circle inside. On fills the track and moves the knob to the end.

  5. 5
    Help.switch__help

    14px, soft ink, under the row, tied to the switch with aria-describedby.

02

Variants

Two, both the settings row. A switch acts the moment it flips, so it lives on a settings screen and never in a form with a Save button: there, it's a checkbox.

2.1

Settings row

A deck setting that changes the next session straight away.

.switch .switch__label .switch__text .switch__control .switch__input .switch__state

role="switch" on a checkbox, so a screen reader says "on" and "off", not "checked". Space flips it, and so does a tap on the words.

2.2

With help

When the name needs a line about what it does.

.switch__help

Under the row, outside the label, tied with aria-describedby. The row above it stays a full 44px target.

03

States

--lift is a 2px hard shadow under the track; --fill inks the track and turns the knob paper. There is no invalid: a switch has no wrong answer.

Off. A framed track, the ink knob at the start, and "Off".

Hover. Anywhere on the row: the track lifts on a 2px hard shadow. Pointers only.

Focus. The hover look, plus a 3px pill-shaped ring 3px out.

On. The track fills with ink, the paper knob moves to the end, and "On".

Pressed. Down and inked, and the knob stretches towards where it's going.

Allow notifications in Settings to get reminders.

Set for every deck in Settings.

Disabled. Dashed and faint, off or on, out of the tab order; the help says why.

The .is-* classes force a state for this page and for tests. They are not API: real states come from :hover (only where a pointer hovers), :focus-visible, :active, :checked and disabled.

04

Usage

  1. Do

    Name the setting, not the action: "Daily reminder", not "Turn on reminders". Make it take effect the moment it flips, and show the effect where it can be seen.

  2. Don't

    Don't put a switch in a form with Save or Cancel: use a checkbox. Don't write On or Off inside the track, and don't let the words beside it be the only change.

  3. Installed PWA

    The whole row is the target, as in iOS Settings. A setting that needs a permission (notifications) asks first, in our words, before the system prompt.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    Space flips it. In SwiftUI, a Toggle with a custom ToggleStyle that draws the pill and the word; on the Mac, system settings in system chrome keep .toggleStyle(.switch).
