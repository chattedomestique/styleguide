Stepper

A small whole number, one step at a time. Two round buttons either side of a value you can also type, and both stop at the limits and say why.

01

Anatomy

Nothing new to draw: Field's label and help, Button's icon-only circles, and Field's input for the value. The stepper only lays them out and keeps them in step.

04

Usage

  1. Do

    Use it for a small range in even steps, where nudging beats typing. Put the range in the help, and name the buttons for what they do: "Fewer cards", "More cards".

  2. Don't

    Don't hide or truly disable a button at a limit: it stays, says why, and keeps focus. Don't use it for a wide range or exact values; that's a number field.

  3. Installed PWA

    The value brings up the number pad. Button's touch-action: manipulation lets quick taps on More cards step instead of zooming the page.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    Up and Down step from the value, as in a Mac number field. In SwiftUI, our circles and a TextField on iOS; the system's Stepper beside the field on the Mac.
