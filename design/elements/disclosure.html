Details

Show more, in place. The browser's own <details>, styled: the whole row opens it, the glyph points the way it went, and it needs no script.

01

Variants

Three parts: the summary (the target), the glyph and the body. A disclosure is a ruled row; stacked, neighbours share a rule, so a group needs no wrapper.

1.1

Single

One thing most people won't need: a how-it-works, a card's notes.

.disclosure .disclosure__summary .disclosure__icon .disclosure__body

The summary is the whole row, at least 44px: words first, the glyph at the end. ic--down swaps to ic--up when it's open, a static glyph, no turn. In a card it takes the card's ink, so it works on every tone.

How spaced repetition works

Cards you know come back less often: tomorrow, then in three days, then in a week. Cards you miss come back sooner, so your time goes where it's needed.

1.2

Group

A list of questions, each answered in place: Help.

.disclosure + .disclosure

Disclosures one after another, each opening on its own. They share their rules, so the group reads as one ruled list. Put a heading over it; the summaries are not headings.

What counts as known?

A card is known once you get it right three sessions running.

Can I study offline?

Yes. Your decks are saved on this device, so a session works anywhere.

Why did a card come back so soon?

You marked it Not yet, so it comes back later in the same session.

How do I start a deck again?

Open the deck, then Settings, then Start again. The cards stay; only what you know is cleared.

1.3

Exclusive group

Steps read one at a time, where two open would only be in the way.

details[name]

The same group with one name on every <details>: opening one closes the others. A progressive extra: where a browser doesn't know name, each opens on its own, which is still fine. Never hide something the reader must compare with another.

03

Usage

  1. Do

    Write the summary as what you'll see: a question, or "Show the answer's notes". Keep what's inside short; a long read deserves its own screen.

  2. Don't

    Don't hide what everyone needs, or an error, or a form's required field. Don't put buttons or links in the summary: the whole row is already one.

  3. Installed PWA

    A tap anywhere on the row opens it; the shell's touchstart listener lets iOS show the press. Nothing moves until gate 4.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    Return or Space opens it. In SwiftUI, DisclosureGroup with a custom DisclosureGroupStyle that draws the row and swaps the glyph; inspectors in system chrome keep the system's triangle.
