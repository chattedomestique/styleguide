Radios

One choice from a short list. A circle, because you act on it; a dot when chosen; and always a question above it, so every option is heard with what it answers.

03

States

The checkbox's states, round. --lift is a 2px hard shadow; --fill inks the circle only while it's pressed. Chosen is the dot: a shape, so it reads without colour.

Rest. A 2px circle on paper.

Hover. Anywhere on the target: the circle lifts on a 2px hard shadow. Pointers only.

Focus. The hover look, plus a round 3px ring 3px out. Arrows move it, and the choice with it.

Chosen. A 10px ink dot. The circle's ground stays paper.

Pressed. Down, and the circle inks over: a solid disc, never mistaken for a dot. A chosen dot turns paper.

04

Usage

  1. Do

    Ask the question in the legend ("Card order") and keep each option to a few words. Order options in a way that means something: the usual first, or least to most.

  2. Don't

    Don't use radios for one yes-or-no: that's a checkbox. Don't go past six options: that's a select. Don't make a radio do something the moment it's chosen.

  3. Installed PWA

    The words are part of the target, so a thumb anywhere on the row chooses. Pressed inks the circle at once, so a tap answers on a screen with no hover.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    Tab reaches the group once; arrows choose. In SwiftUI, a Picker with .pickerStyle(.radioGroup) on the Mac; on iOS a custom row with our circle, since iOS has no native radio.
