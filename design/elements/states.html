States

Empty, loading and error: what a screen shows before there's anything, and when something fails. Never blank, and always a way forward.

01

Patterns

Six, made of elements that already exist: Card, Button, Spinner, Banner, Field, and Frame with its Top bar and Tab bar. The only new class is .state, which keeps a state's words next to its way forward. Each is shown on the Decks screen of a phone; the markup printed is the pattern's own.

1.1

Empty

Nothing here yet: the first time a screen has nothing to show.

.state .card--ghost

One line that says so, and a ghost card in the place the first deck will go: dashed means nothing here yet, and the whole card is the link to the first step. Its name is the step, "Make your first deck", not the state.

Failed

It didn't load, and there's nothing to show instead.

.card .card__foot role="alert"

A plain card where the content would be: what happened as its title, what to do if trying again doesn't work, and Try again in its foot. An alert, because nothing on the screen works until it does. 02

States

One screen moves between the patterns. What shows, what a screen reader hears, and where focus is at each moment. Nothing moves on screen until gate 4: each appears in place.

  1. Opening, the first 300ms

    Shows: Nothing new: the bars are already there.

    Heard: Nothing.

    Focus: Where it was.

  2. Still loading

    Shows: The spinner and “Loading decks…” where the content will be.

    Heard: “Loading decks…”, politely, once.

    Focus: Where it was.

  3. Loaded

    Shows: The decks, in place of the spinner.

    Heard: Nothing: they're there when you look.

    Focus: Where it was.

  4. Loaded, nothing there

    Shows: “No decks yet.” and the ghost card.

    Heard: Nothing.

    Focus: Where it was; the ghost card is the next stop.

  5. Didn't load

    Shows: The plain card, Try again in its foot.

    Heard: At once (role="alert").

    Focus: Where it was; Try again is the next stop.

  6. Trying again

    Shows: The card stays; Try again goes busy, its label kept.

    Heard: Nothing.

    Focus: On Try again. When the decks come, on the scroll region (the screen's <main>), so it isn't lost.

  7. Searching, no match

    Shows: The no-match line and Clear search, under the field.

    Heard: The line, politely, each time the list changes.

    Focus: In the field.

  8. Search cleared

    Shows: The field empties; the decks come back.

    Heard: Nothing.

    Focus: Back in the field.

  9. Offline

    Shows: The offline banner first, the decks on this device under it.

    Heard: “You're offline. Changes are saved on this device.”, politely, once.

    Focus: Where it was.

  10. Online again

    Shows: The banner goes.

    Heard: Nothing.

    Focus: Where it was.

  11. First run

    Shows: One line, one action, no bars to wander off with.

    Heard: The heading and the line, read in order.

    Focus: The action is the first stop.

At 200% text each pattern wraps and grows taller, never wider. In forced colours the cards' and the banner's frames and rules, the ghost card's dashes and every glyph stay, in system colours.

03

Usage

  1. Do

    Say what's going on, where the content would be, and offer the way forward. Keep the bars: a state is the content of a screen, never a screen without its way back.

  2. Don't

    Don't leave a screen blank, don't shimmer or draw skeleton boxes, don't say "Something went wrong" on its own, and don't say one failure twice (a banner and a card).

  3. Installed PWA

    Never blank (STYLE.md §4): empty is a ghost card offering the first step; an error is a plain card with Try again in its foot. Offline is not an error: show what's on the device, under the banner.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    The same patterns in the window's content. SwiftUI's ContentUnavailableView is the native one; we draw ours from the cards instead, because it centres a large symbol, which is decoration here. Loading is a ProgressView with its words.
