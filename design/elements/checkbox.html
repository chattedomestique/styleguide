Checkbox

Independent on/off choices. A square box, because every platform draws a checkbox square; the words are part of the target; and checked carries a mark, so it never needs colour.

01

Anatomy

A box inside a label, and Field's label, help and error around a group, in Field's order. Only the label and the box are always there.

02

Variants

Four, all the same box. A checkbox is a choice you make now and that counts when the form is sent; a setting that acts the moment you touch it is a switch.

2.1

Single

One choice, yes or no.

.checkbox .checkbox__label .checkbox__input

The words say what checked means: "Show hints", not "Hints". The input sits inside its label, so the label needs no for.

2.2

Group

Several choices that answer one question.

.checkbox-group .field__label .field__help

A <fieldset> with a <legend>, one name for all the boxes. Each box is its own tab stop; Space toggles it. Options are 8px apart, so two 44px targets never touch.

2.3

Mixed

"All decks", over a group where only some are checked.

:indeterminate aria-controls

Mixed exists only in script: input.indeterminate = true, and screen readers hear "mixed". The box lists the boxes it controls in aria-controls. Checking it checks them all; a press in the mixed state checks all, as natively. Try it: this one is live.

03

States

Two numbers draw them, as on the card: --lift is a 2px hard shadow, --fill is the box's ground. Both ease; the glyph and the ring don't wait. Checked and mixed carry a glyph too, so each state reads in grey and in forced colours.

Rest. A 2px square frame on paper.

Hover. Anywhere on the target: the box lifts on a 2px hard shadow. Pointers only.

Focus. The hover look, plus a 3px ring 3px out, square like the box.

Checked. Filled with ink, the check in paper: a mark, not only a colour.

Pressed. Down, filled, with a paper ring inside the frame, so a tap answers before the finger lifts.

Mixed. Filled, with the minus: some of the decks under it are checked.

04

Usage

  1. Do

    Say what checked means, in the positive: "Show hints", not "Hide hints" or "Hints". Put a group's question in its legend, and use a checkbox when the choice counts on Save.

  2. Don't

    Don't use a checkbox for something that happens at once: that's a switch. Don't use one for a single choice from a list: that's a radio. Don't round it, and don't put the help inside the label.

  3. Installed PWA

    The whole label is the target, so a thumb on the words toggles it. Pressed fills the box at once, so a tap answers on a screen with no hover.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    Space toggles; Tab moves box to box. In SwiftUI, a Toggle with a custom ToggleStyle that draws the square; on the Mac, .toggleStyle(.checkbox) in system chrome.
