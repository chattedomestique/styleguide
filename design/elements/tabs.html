Tabs

Sections of one screen, one at a time: a deck's Cards, Stats and Settings. Without script they are links to sections stacked down the page, so nothing is lost.

01

Anatomy

A row of tabs on a rule, and under it the section they show. Not the segmented control: that switches how one thing is shown; tabs switch which section is shown.

Cards 7 3 4 Stats 2 Settings 1

Twelve cards. Five known, seven due today.

  1. 1
    List.tabs__list

    A <nav> named for the screen; the script makes it the tablist. A row on a 1px rule. It wraps rather than scrolls: a hidden scrollbar hides tabs.

  2. 2
    Tab.tabs__tab

    A link to its section; the script makes it a tab. A pill target, 44px tall, 16px a side, body type at 600, soft ink. Quiet until hover, like a quiet button.

  3. 3
    Selected[aria-selected="true"]

    Ink words and a 2px bar on the rule under the tab. A flat bar, not a fill: a fill is a press, and a round ring is focus.

  4. 4
    Count.badge

    Tag and Badge's badge, after the label. aria-hidden: the count is in the tab's name ("Cards, 7 due").

  5. 5
    Panel.tabs__panel .tabs__heading

    A <section> with its own heading; the script makes it a tabpanel labelled by its tab, one tab stop, and keeps the heading for headings navigation, out of sight.

02

Variants

All live: click a tab, or Tab to the row and use the arrows, Home and End. One tab stop for the row, one for the panel.

2.1

Two to five tabs

A screen with a few sections that are never needed at once: inside a deck.

.tabs .tabs__list .tabs__tab .tabs__panel .tabs__heading

The markup is links to sections; js/components/tabs.js, loaded after sheet.js, makes ARIA tabs of it. The first tab is selected, or the one whose section is in the address (#tabs-deck-stats). One tab means no tabs; six means the sections want their own screens.

2.2

Full width

On a phone: the tabs share the whole row.

.tabs--full

Equal shares while the labels fit; a label never gives up its own width, so long words or large text wrap the row onto a second line instead of squeezing a word. The bar is the whole share's width.

2.3

With a count

When a section has something waiting: the cards due today.

.badge

The badge follows the label, as in a text button, and is aria-hidden; the tab's aria-label carries the count ("Cards, 7 due") and starts with the visible label, so voice control still finds it. Capped at "99+".

The .is-* classes force a state for this page and for tests. They are not API: real states come from aria-selected (set by the script), :hover (only where a pointer hovers), :focus-visible and :active.

04

Usage

  1. Do

    Use two to five tabs for sections of one screen, one word each. Give every section a heading, so the page still reads without script.

  2. Don't

    Don't use tabs to filter or re-show one thing (Grid or List, Front or Back): that's the segmented control. Don't use them to go to another screen: that's the tab bar or a link.

  3. Installed PWA

    Full width on a phone. The selected tab lives in the address (#tabs-deck-stats), so a deep link, a shared link or a reload opens the right section.

  4. Mac and SwiftUI

    On the Mac, Tab reaches the row and arrows move along it, as in a native tab view. In SwiftUI, a custom strip of Buttons with the selected trait over a switch of views; a system TabView in a window is the system's.
