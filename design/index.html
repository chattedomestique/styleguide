System

Everything our installed phone apps and Mac apps are built from: seven rules, one set of tokens, and the elements, each through six gates.

00

Ground rules

Seven rules. Each one is checkable, and tools/check.mjs checks the ones a machine can.

  1. Two line weights.

    2px frames what you press or what contains. 1px only divides. A heavier 4px exists for forced-colours and nothing else.

  2. Flat.

    No gradients, no blur, no glass. A shadow is hard-edged and means “you can press this”. Hover and press never move the hit box.

  3. Rectangles hold, circles act.

    Containers are square rectangles. Buttons and handles are circles or pills; the one exception is the bar's small square control, which belongs to the frame.

  4. Uppercase is structure.

    Bar labels and index numbers. Everything you read is sentence case.

  5. Say something real.

    No lorem ipsum, no “John Doe”, no “seamless”. Copy comes from the product.

  6. Colour is a swap.

    Components read roles: --paper, --ink, --tone-3. Only tokens/color.*.css knows a hex.

  7. No decoration.

    If it carries no information and offers no action, it goes. No dotted backgrounds, no icons in tinted circles.

Elements

Everything a phone app or a Mac app needs, in the order it's being built. Each element gets a page here and a spec in elements/. Colour, motion and the freeze then pass over all of them together.
